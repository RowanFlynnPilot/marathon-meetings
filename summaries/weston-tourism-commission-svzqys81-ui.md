## Meeting Overview
The Village of Weston Tourism Commission met on October 20, 2025, acknowledging a strong summer tourism season highlighted by the World Horseshoe Tournament, which generated $3.2 million in direct visitor spending and $5.1 million in economic impact. Two grant requests — the 2026 Fall Cup post-event request and a tree-clearing project on the Eau Claire River — were tabled until the January 2027 meeting due to missing post-action reports and absent applicants. Several post-event reports were acknowledged and a suggestion was raised to require revenue/expense summaries in future post-event reporting.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comment
Jim Pensel, 500 [street inaudible] Weston, raised two concerns: first, that the meeting packet layout was confusing; second, that a post-event request for the Fall Cup soccer event was submitted without a concurrent after-action report even though the event had already occurred. He also questioned the realism of room estimates in some applications, noting the Village of Weston only has 317 hotel rooms, and urged that future applications specify rooms impacted within Weston specifically.

### September Budget Status Report
The commission acknowledged a budget status report showing revenues and expenditures through the second quarter. A member noted the account balance stood at $233,531. No questions were raised and the report was acknowledged by motion.

### CVB Report
CVB representative Tim reported a highly successful summer tourism season, led by the World Horseshoe Tournament, which drew approximately 3,000 participants and families over two-plus weeks, generated $3.2 million in direct visitor spending and $5.1 million in economic impact, and brought hotel occupancy in the area to 86.2%. Tim noted discussions are underway with World Horseshoe leadership about potentially hosting again in 2033 and 2037 without a formal bid process. Marathon County's total annual tourism economic impact was cited at over $520 million with 2 million-plus visitors. Discussion also touched on the lack of new grant applications ahead of the upcoming funding cycle and whether posting meeting dates publicly on the CVB website would help applicants meet deadlines.

### Grant Request — 2026 Fall Cup Post-Event Request
Commissioners noted applicant Dan was not present to explain the late submission. Tim confirmed the CVB had no communication from Dan regarding the application or his absence. Members expressed concern about setting a precedent by approving after-the-fact requests without adequate documentation, though several acknowledged the Fall Cup is a significant event. The commission voted unanimously to table the item until January, directing the CVB to contact Dan and require his presence at the next meeting along with a post-action report.

### Grant Request — Clearing of Trees from Eau Claire River
The applicant was not present. One commissioner shared a personal account of a dangerous incident on the river caused by downed trees, supporting the safety rationale for the project, and noted the trees had already been cut. However, commissioners agreed they lacked sufficient detail — including how many trees were removed, photos, and a post-action report — to act. The item was tabled until January with a request for pictures and a full post-action report.

### Post-Event Reports (Items 9A–9E): Wings over Wisconsin Wausau, Fourth of July, Weston Acoustic Concert Series, Bluesfest, World Horseshoe Tournament
Commissioners reviewed five post-event reports. Questions were raised about the Wings over Wisconsin Wausau report regarding what the 'value amount' column represented (clarified as marketing expenditures, not revenues) and whether the event's financials were disclosed. A suggestion was made to add a revenues-versus-expenses requirement to post-event reporting forms, with the chair directing this be placed as a separate action item for January. All five post-event reports were acknowledged by a single motion, passed unanimously.

### Staff Remarks and Future Meeting
Staff reminded commissioners that a Halloween map is currently active for residents to submit Halloween activities, and that the end-of-year farmers market survey is open through October 30th. The next meeting was set for January 18th, 2027, at 4 p.m., with the two tabled grant items and a potential policy vote on post-event financial reporting to be added to the agenda.
