## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Joint Extension, Education, Economic Development and Human Resources, Finance, and Property Committees met to consider a one-year operating lease for the former UWSP campus theater building with the Marathon Center for Arts and Culture (MCAC), and to review two new proposals for the River Drive properties. The committees approved the MCAC lease with a modification requiring the tenant to cover approximately $7,428 in annual building insurance costs, then voted to enter closed session to discuss negotiation strategy on the River Drive RFP responses.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comment
Two speakers offered public comment. Jim Davo, Village Administrator of Kronenwetter, thanked county staff in the clerk's office, highway shop, and sheriff's department for their professionalism and cooperative work with the village. Gail Marshall, speaking by phone, urged the committees to reject the current RFP proposals for the River Drive properties, arguing the process had failed to produce meaningful results and recommending the county instead hire a commercial real estate broker — estimating a 3% commission would still yield 97% of sale proceeds to taxpayers. She also asked what the county currently insures the River Drive properties for.

### Approval of September 9, 2026 Joint Meeting Minutes
Minutes were approved by both committees in separate motions. For the Extension, Education, and Economic Development (Triple-ED) committee, Supervisor Hagen moved and the motion was seconded; it passed. For the HR, Finance, and Property (HRFC) committee, Vice Chair Pool moved and Supervisor Watson seconded; it passed.

### UWSP Campus Redevelopment Update and MCAC One-Year Lease
Administrator Chris Holman reported that a city feasibility study agreement is in place, building systems are being inventoried and winterized, and staff are working with the UW exit committee. The proposed one-year operating lease with the Marathon Center for Arts and Culture (MCAC) was presented to bridge the gap while MCAC finalizes its 501c3 property tax exemption with the state. Tim Parker, CEO of the Community Foundation of North Central Wisconsin and MCAC fiscal agent, told committees that 95% of office spaces are leased and theater bookings are at 60% through June, with January and February already full. Kurt Gibbs raised the issue of insurance costs, noting the building is insured for approximately $13 million and the annual premium is roughly $7,428; he argued that cost should be borne by the tenant. Vice Chair Pool moved to approve the lease with a modification directing staff to negotiate inclusion of the $7,428 insurance cost spread over monthly payments; the motion was seconded and passed.

### River Drive RFP Proposals – Raise and Roast Cafe (Oswald)
Justin and Elena Oswald presented their proposal to purchase 210 River Drive for $1 to develop 'Raise and Roast Cafe,' envisioning an affordable community cafe on the ground floor and a fundraising event venue on the second floor, with free recreational activities and partnerships with downtown institutions. Holman noted the proposal has strong alignment with criteria for activating unused property, community benefit, and local ownership, with moderate alignment on job creation and tax base reactivation. No formal vote or counter-offer was made in open session.

### River Drive RFP Proposals – VFW Post 388 Veterans and Community Campus
Tamara Sillers, VFW Post 388 events coordinator, presented the VFW's proposal to acquire all three River Drive buildings (210, 212, and 326) to create a veterans and community campus, with 210 as an event venue, 212 as a community lounge with mental health counseling space and bingo, and 326 as garage and storage. She noted the VFW vacated River Drive around 2017 and the move was a mistake for community connectivity. Supervisors questioned how the new model differs from the bar-and-restaurant model that led to the prior departure, the organization's current revenue sources, and its nonprofit status (501c19). Holman disclosed the appraised market value of the River Drive parcel at $2,268,000 (land $87,000, improvements $1,261,000 as of November 2025) and insurance values: 210 at $2,635,700, 212 at $3,734,400, and 326 at $1,271,100. The presiding chair expressed disappointment at dollar-sale proposals given significant county investment, stating an obligation to taxpayers to maximize return.

### Motion to Enter Closed Session on River Drive RFP
Corp Counsel explained that closed session was appropriate to discuss negotiation strategy and potential counter-proposals without revealing the county's position to the proposing parties. Both committees voted separately by roll call to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin Statute 19.85(1)(e) for competitive or bargaining reasons, specifically consideration of RFP responses for River Drive property. Both roll call votes passed unanimously.
