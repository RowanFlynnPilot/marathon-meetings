## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Human Resources Committee met on October 12th and took action on two items: approving a request for an additional HR staff member (to be incorporated into the 2027 budget rather than a budget modification), and voting to maintain the existing summer hours schedule and traditional full-week winter hours rather than adopting year-round summer hours. The HR staffing approval is significant as a previous attempt to add the position had failed at common council.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of Minutes from Preceding Meetings
Alder Wowski moved to approve the minutes from preceding meetings and Alder Larson seconded. The motion passed on a voice vote with no opposition.

### Reintroduction of Request to Approve Additional HR Department Staff Member
The acting HR director presented a detailed case for an additional HR coordinator, citing 24 active workers' compensation cases, complex FMLA administration, and an overall unsustainable workload. Alder Killian expressed full support, saying the workload 'looks unsustainable.' Alder Martins moved for approval, seconded by Alder Buscowski, and the motion passed. Discussion followed about timing: a prior budget modification had already been approved by finance and sent to common council but did not pass, so the committee agreed the position would instead be built into the 2027 budget, with posting anticipated on or after January 2nd.

### Recommendation to Establish Summer Hours as Year-Round City Hall Schedule
HR staff proposed making summer hours (Monday–Thursday 7:15 a.m.–4:30 p.m., half-day Friday) permanent year-round, noting that extended Tuesday evening hours attracted only five visitors all summer, three of whom came between 5:00 and 6:00 p.m. Alder Killian opposed year-round summer hours, arguing the city has a responsibility to be open during standard business hours including Friday afternoons, and moved to keep summer hours in summer while restoring full business hours in winter with one extended evening; that motion received no second. Alder Martins raised concern about closing Friday afternoons in winter and suggested a pilot for evening hours. Alder Larson moved to keep summer hours as summer hours and restore traditional full five-day winter hours, seconded by Alder Martins; that motion passed on a voice vote.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.

## Action Items
- Additional HR staff member position approved; to be incorporated into the 2027 budget with posting anticipated on or after January 2nd rather than via immediate budget modification
- Summer hours schedule maintained for summer months; traditional full five-day business-hours schedule maintained for winter months — year-round summer hours proposal rejected
