## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works met on November 17th and handled three items: approving prior meeting minutes, opening 15 bids for the 2026 demolition of the former MBX South building at 207 North 1st Avenue (with no award made pending funding confirmation), and approving a contractor pay estimate before moving into closed session. The demolition bid opening is significant as it represents a major redevelopment step for the former MBX South site downtown.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of November 10th Meeting Minutes
A board member moved to approve the minutes and another seconded. The motion passed on a voice vote with all members in favor.

### 2026 Demolition of 207 North 1st Avenue (Former MBX South) — Bid Opening
Staff (identified as Patrick) read 15 bids aloud, ranging from a low of $150,000 (WS Trucking and Construction) to a high of $440,362.70 (RC Pavers). The apparent low bidder was WS Trucking and Construction at $150,000. No award was made at this time; Patrick explained that community development must first work through funding to confirm the project is ready to proceed, and the item will be brought back to the board.

### 2026 Sidewalk Repair Project A — Pember Company Pay Estimate No. 3
Staff presented pay estimate number three from Pember Company in the amount of $8,285 for work to date, as approved by project inspector Matt Guptail. A board member moved to approve the payment, another seconded, and the motion passed unanimously on a voice vote.

### Closed Session — Deliberation on Claims
The board voted to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin State Statutes 19.85(1)(g) for the purpose of deliberating on claims. The motion was seconded and approved on a voice vote.

No public comment was offered.
