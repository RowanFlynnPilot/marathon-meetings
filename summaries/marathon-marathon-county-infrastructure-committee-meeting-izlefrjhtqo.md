## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Infrastructure Committee held a brief meeting covering several operational items including a resolution recognizing Crash Responder Safety Week, a quick claim deed transfer of county-owned right-of-way property to a local township, a strategic plan public survey update, and a detailed update on the highway shop construction project. The highway shop project bid opening was moved to October 5th due to high bidder interest, with the county board education meeting on the project scheduled for October 15th and a guaranteed maximum price contract expected to come before the full county board later in October.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of September 9th and September 17th, 2026 Meeting Minutes
Minutes were approved with a motion by Griner and a second by Coyle. Chair Gibbs noted that a couple of items from the last meeting were not discussed and said he received a call regarding the safety routes program in Corona, requesting it be added to the next agenda.

### Resolution Recognizing November 2nd–6th as Crash Responder Safety Week
A motion was made by a member identified as Kelly to forward the resolution to the county board, seconded by another member. The resolution was approved unanimously without significant debate.

### Transfer of Property Adjacent to County Trunk Highway via Quit Claim Deed
Staff explained this was a legacy co-ownership issue where the county and a town jointly held a right-of-way parcel, and that Commissioner Lang worked with the town to resolve it by transferring the county's interest back to local control via quit claim deed of Lot 1 of CSM 87-79. A motion to recommend approval to the county board was made by Coyle and seconded by Griner, passing unanimously with no discussion.

### Strategic Plan Development Update Including Public Survey Opportunity
Staff described a forthcoming public online survey narrowed to the top 20 priorities already identified by the county board and department heads from the comprehensive plan, aimed at further guiding the strategic plan. Supervisor Dickinson, participating online, questioned how the survey would reach county residents, expressing skepticism about whether a small number of responses (he estimated around 1,400) would meaningfully guide committee work, and asked how infrastructure-specific objectives would be distinguished. Staff said the approach would mirror the county's life report process using press releases, the county website, and outreach at town meetings, and acknowledged that ultimately the board and standing committees would need to keep the strategic plan at the forefront of their discussions.

### CIT Project Update
Staff noted that the relevant staff member was not present and there was no update in the packet. Members were directed to contact staff directly with any specific project questions.

### Year-to-Date Budget Report
Finance staff reported that through June, the highway department was at 68.67% of revenue budget and 40.16% of expenditure budget, with payroll at 43.24% against a 46.74% benchmark, noting no expenditure concerns. Supervisor Griner asked about fuel cost impacts in the current quarter; staff said the impacts were not yet known but acknowledged the county could be affected.

### Highway Shop Project Update
Staff reported that site plan approval and the conditional use permit through the Village of Kronau were recently completed. The bid package has been on the street since September 8th, with the bid opening moved from September 29th to October 5th at 2 p.m. at the Lake View Conference Center due to high bidder interest — approximately 80 to 90 questions were fielded from prospective bidders. The construction manager is Miron Construction. Staff said a guaranteed maximum price would be prepared for presentation at the county board education meeting on October 15th, with a formal contract vote expected at the county board's voting meeting later in October. Supervisor Griner asked about the bid unsealing time; staff confirmed 2 p.m.

### 2027 Budget Announcement
An administrator (not a committee member) announced that the proposed 2027 budget had been delivered to the HR Finance and Property Committee and is available online. He noted that any supervisor wishing to propose amendments must submit them by October 9th ahead of the HR Finance and Property Committee meeting on October 12th, which will authorize the budget for publication. The formal county board adoption meeting is scheduled for November 10th. The highway facility project was described as a cornerstone of the budget presentation.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered. The chair noted no one had registered to speak during the public comment period.
