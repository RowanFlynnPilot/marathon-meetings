## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Finance Committee met on October 27, 2026, receiving a clean audit opinion from new auditing firm Maldin Jenkins CPAs for fiscal year 2025, with one minor single audit finding related to suspension and debarment recordkeeping. The committee approved several items including a federal justice grant, a TIF development payment, airport hangar lease increases, and a fire department apprentice program budget amendment, while tabling the Wausau Cemetery Association agreement pending cost estimates and additional information.

## Key Discussions
### Presentation of 2025 Financial and Single Audit by Maldin Jenkins CPAs
Lucas Hand, CPA and director at Maldin Jenkins, presented the city's fiscal year 2025 audit results, reporting a clean, unmodified opinion on both the financial statements and the single audit compliance report. The city ended the year with total net position of $300.3 million, general fund revenues of $46.3 million versus expenditures of $47 million, and an overall net position increase of $12.7 million. One single audit finding was noted — a significant deficiency for suspension and debarment recordkeeping, which Hand described as a common checklist issue with no impact on federal funds. Hand also issued a management point (not an audit finding) recommending cross-training and documentation of key processes, noting that finance department knowledge is concentrated in a limited number of individuals. Alderperson Hincki asked Hand to expand on succession planning concerns, and the chair asked about upcoming GASB standards 103 and 104, with Hand expressing confidence the city is prepared.

### Approving Fiscal Year 2026 Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant and Interlocal Agreement with Marathon County
Captain Graham presented the annual formula-based federal grant of $15,920, awarded jointly to the City of Wausau and Marathon County, requiring an interlocal agreement for Marathon County to receive funds. The grant is historically used to fund equipment in the regional forensic lab, such as GrayKey or Cellebrite technology. No significant debate occurred and the item was approved unanimously.

### Approving Budget Amendment for 415 South 1st Avenue Development Agreement with Gorman Company (Westside Commons)
Patrick from economic development explained that Gorman Company had met all milestones under their development agreement, including minimum spend, project completion, and obtaining 90% occupancy for three or more months, as well as securing permanent financing. The committee approved a $650,000 Tax Increment Grant (TIG) payment to Gorman Company, which will come before the full council as a budget modification. The vote carried without notable debate.

### Authorizing Staff to Prepare Project Plan Amendment for Tax Increment District 8
The chair described this item as moving private-side lead service line replacement costs to TID 8 where appropriate, along with 17th Avenue reconstruction and asphalt overlay within the district — topics previously discussed during budget sessions. The motion passed without discussion.

### Approving Agreement Between City of Wausau and Wausau Cemetery Association
Staff presented a revised, consolidated cemetery agreement reflecting a current annual payment of $76,721.44 and new language giving the public works director authority to offer surplus vehicles to the cemetery at no cost on a right-of-first-refusal basis. Parks Director Jamie raised significant concerns about a new, large tree removal and stump grinding request from the cemetery — nine trees and 23 total stumps — which would take city urban forestry crews approximately two weeks, raising questions about liability for headstone damage, worker safety on non-city property, and cost. Alder Watson moved to table the agreement until the next finance committee meeting pending cost estimates, a map of the requested work, copies of the prior agreements, and potential cemetery board attendance; Alder Hincki seconded. The motion passed with one nay and the chair abstaining, citing his wife's service on the cemetery board. Items G and H were also pulled to the next meeting.

### Approving 3% Increase to Hangar Lease Agreements at Wausau Downtown Airport Beginning 2027
The committee discussed a 3% rate increase for T-hangar leases at Wausau Downtown Airport, with current monthly rates ranging from approximately $3 to $6 depending on hangar size. Staff noted the airport is below market compared to peer facilities. Alder Hincki asked for more detail on how rates are set and how far behind market the city is. Alder Neil moved to approve; Alder Hincki seconded. The item passed unanimously.

### Approving Multi-Year Budget Amendment for Wausau Fire Department Apprentice Program Paid by Federal Grant
Fire Chief Jeremy explained this was a bookkeeping amendment to create a budget line matching the previously approved federal apprenticeship grant, enabling staff to track spending across the three-year grant period. He confirmed all costs — including gear, equipment, and collaboration with Northcentral Technical College — are covered within the grant for two cohorts of nine apprentices each. Alder Hincki asked whether apprentices would have a direct path to city employment; the chief clarified they would need to apply for open positions through the standard process. The motion from Hincki with a second from Tyranny passed unanimously.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered. The chair noted only staff were present when public comment was called.
