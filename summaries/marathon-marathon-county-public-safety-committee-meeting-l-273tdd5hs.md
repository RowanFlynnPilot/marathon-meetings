## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Public Safety Committee met to discuss the ongoing Rib Mountain Law Enforcement Services contract negotiations, receive a 2027 budget overview for departments under committee jurisdiction, and address procedural disputes over meeting minutes. No final votes were taken on the Rib Mountain contract, which remains in negotiation with a follow-up meeting between county administration, the sheriff, and Rib Mountain representatives scheduled for the following week.

## Key Discussions
### Minutes Approval Dispute
A supervisor (identified as Lance or Joel in context) disputed whether prior requests to add drones and flock cameras to the agenda had been recorded in committee minutes. Corporate counsel advised that minutes could only be amended to reflect requests made during the specific meeting in question, and that reconsidering already-approved minutes would require a formal motion for reconsideration. The committee agreed by consensus to defer approval of the current minutes pending chair review of video from the previous meeting to verify accuracy.

### Rib Mountain Law Enforcement Services Contract Update
County Administrator Leonard reported that negotiations with Rib Mountain are ongoing and productive, with a follow-up meeting scheduled the following week involving the sheriff and Rib Mountain representatives. The current draft contract contemplates a fourth deputy in April of the following year, a fifth to be considered based on data review, and a goal of a 90/10 split for call response — meaning 90% of Rib Mountain calls answered by Rib Mountain-dedicated deputies. The full contract value is approximately $593,667.96. Supervisor Griner questioned the process and suggested the sheriff should be the one initiating staffing requests; Supervisors Robinson and Boots defended the data-driven county-initiated approach. Captain Sybert confirmed Rib Mountain is commercially heavy with high retail theft calls. The committee was told the contract must ultimately be ratified by the full county board.

### Strategic Plan Public Survey
Board Vice Chair Boots briefly noted that the strategic plan survey is now open to the general public rather than targeted groups as in prior cycles, and encouraged committee members to urge constituents to participate. He indicated the committee would examine strategic plan results more closely in December after the budget process concludes.

### 2027 Budget Overview for Committee Jurisdictions
Administrator Leonard highlighted key budget changes: the Clerk of Courts has anticipated new revenue from Wisconsin Act 179; the District Attorney's office faces a roughly $125,000 revenue reduction due to the state public defender's office lowering discovery payments statewide; the Medical Examiner's office abolished a deputy medical examiner position and created a senior administrative position, reducing overall expenditures; and the Sheriff's Office budget increase nearly accounts for all of the county's net new construction revenue, with $531,000 in attrition savings budgeted and increased rolling stock funding to address squad shortfalls from 2026. Supervisor Brandon asked about using opioid settlement funds to offset sheriff department costs such as Narcan and treatment programs; Leonard confirmed this is a viable strategy and favored a trust model for perpetual benefit, though no opioid settlement spending is included in the 2027 budget. Supervisor Tim requested cost information on flock cameras; Leonard indicated they are currently grant-funded but would confirm with the sheriff.

### Future Agenda Items — Drones and Flock/Axon Cameras
Supervisor Griner formally requested that drones and flock/Axon camera systems be placed on the next meeting agenda, including costs, number of units, locations, contractual terms, and early termination provisions. He expressed concern about undisclosed camera placements. Administrator Leonard noted a prior full presentation on license plate readers occurred at the August meeting. Sheriff Chad [last name inaudible] arrived during this discussion and was made aware of the request.
