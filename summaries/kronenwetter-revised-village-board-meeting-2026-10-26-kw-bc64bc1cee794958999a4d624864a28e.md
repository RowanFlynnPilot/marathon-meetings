## Meeting Overview
The Kronenwetter Village Board held its October 26, 2026 meeting with six of seven trustees present, taking action on several items including a zoning change request, new municipal center hours, and retaining Ordinance 14-16 as written. Finance Director John provided a detailed TID closure update projecting significant future tax levy benefits, while a public commenter raised concerns about the Green Tree development and well contamination near the project site.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comment - Green Tree Development
Bruny Kramer of 2150 East State Highway 153, Schofield, addressed the board criticizing the Green Tree development decision, alleging the village did not follow Kronenwetter in-place standards and that no one is accountable for the decision. He also reported that a well service person told him a well on the corner of Pine and Pleasant had 40 feet of contamination and required a full system replacement costing over $10,000, which Kramer said was located near the planned development site.

### Finance Director/Treasurer's Report - TID Update
Finance Director John provided an extensive update on Tax Increment District closures, projecting that TID 3 is already in closure mode adding $4,139 to the 2027 tax levy, TID 2 could close five years early in 2029 releasing $80 million in valuation, TID 4 could close two years early in 2032, and TID 1 could close by 2039 with the distribution center coming online. He projected cumulative annual tax levy benefits of up to $400,000-$441,000 once all TIDs are closed, calling it major future benefit to village operations, debt service, and capital projects.

### Consent Agenda - Item E (Vouchers/ACH/Credit Cards) and Item F (Operator License)
The board separated the consent agenda after trustee questions. Regarding Item E, Trustee Coyle noted approximately $41,000 in legal costs over 2026 related to open records requests from a specific individual; Finance Director John confirmed the line item on page 13-14 was for one person's requests and pledged a full cost breakdown at the November meeting. Item F, an operator's license application with a missing or incorrect item on the form, was directed back to the clerk for the applicant to correct and resubmit rather than being approved or denied, consistent with prior board practice. Item E was approved 6-0 on roll call; Item F was not voted on and was returned to staff.

### Zoning Change Request - 3115 Aspen Road (Boshensky)
The board considered a request by Ms. Boshensky to rezone 2.89 acres at 3115 Aspen Road from R-5 to R-2. There was no notable debate. Trustee Lesniak moved to approve both the zoning change and the accompanying ordinance; Trustee Stowell seconded. The motion passed 6-0 on roll call.

### Municipal Center Hours
The board discussed a proposed change to municipal center public hours: 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Monday through Thursday, and 7:30 a.m. to noon on Fridays (with staff working internally through 4:30 on Fridays), year-round. Finance Director John raised a concern about December tax collection season and Friday hours but said he would discuss with staff and the administrator before proposing any changes. The board noted that by-appointment access on Fridays has been accommodated previously. Trustee Mortensen moved to approve the new schedule; Trustee Stowell seconded. Motion passed by voice vote.

### Ordinance 14-16 - Police and Fire Commission Composition
President Joling acknowledged he had made an appointment to the Police and Fire Commission without accounting for the ordinance's composition requirements, resulting in the appointee also serving on the Utility Commission in violation of the ordinance. The board discussed and agreed not to change the ordinance; instead, President Joling will ask the individual (identified as Alex) to choose which commission he wishes to remain on, and a new appointment will be sought for the other. Trustee Coyle moved to keep Ordinance 14-16 as written; motion was seconded and passed by voice vote.

### Legal Services Renewal - Wolfgram Agreement
Finance Director John presented a proposed renewal of the municipal court legal services agreement with attorney Wolfgram, increasing the rate from $185 to $200 per hour (8.1% increase) locked in for three years. Trustee Solheim raised a concern about the 10-year records retention requirement in Section 5 of the agreement, questioning whether it differed from prior contract language and noting cost implications. The board agreed to table the item and bring it back at the next meeting to allow Clerk Noak time to compare the language with the prior agreement, noting a 60-day termination notice deadline of approximately November 1.

## Public Comment
One speaker offered public comment: Bruny Kramer of 2150 East State Highway 153 criticized the Green Tree development decision, alleging the village failed to follow its own in-place standards, that no one faces consequences, and that a well at the corner of Pine and Pleasant had been severely contaminated, requiring a full replacement costing over $10,000 — located near the proposed development.
