## Meeting Overview
The Wausau City Council held a joint Human Resources and Finance Committee meeting to consider the 2027 employee benefit design, which includes significant increases to deductibles and insurance premiums. Both committees approved the benefit package as presented after debate over the financial burden on employees, and the HR committee then moved into closed session to consider a union grievance regarding an employee termination.

## Key Discussions
### 2027 Benefit Design Approval
Alan Wussellowski, city engineer with 26 years of service, addressed the committees expressing concern that the family deductible increase from $3,400 to $6,000 represents a roughly $3,000 per year additional burden on employees and warned it would hinder recruitment, citing a city surveyor position that has gone unfilled for two years with no applicants. HR Director Keenan responded that the city went to market and the Aspirus plan was the best available, noting the original premium increase proposed was 20.3% and was negotiated down to 9.6% in part through the deductible increases; she also noted the county's deductibles are higher ($4,000 single / $6,500 family) and city employees pay only 12% of premiums versus the county's 15%. Alder Hickeyi raised that the deductible increase for families represents roughly a 76% increase and questioned whether the city could increase HSA contributions, to which staff indicated that adding $600 per family plan would cost approximately $101,400 more on top of the already $550,000 premium increase, totaling over $650,000 in additional budget exposure. Keenan noted that 75% of employees with health insurance do not currently reach the maximum out-of-pocket and that the city also provides a 3% COLA plus merit increases, which is better than many employers.

### Finance Committee Vote on 2027 Benefit Package
Alder Neil moved to approve the benefit package as presented on the finance side; Alder Tierney seconded. The motion carried on a voice vote with no audible opposition.

### HR Committee Vote on 2027 Benefit Package
A motion was made to approve the benefit package as presented on the HR side; Alder Kilian seconded. The motion passed on a voice vote with no audible opposition.

### Finance Committee Adjournment from Joint Session
Alder Neil moved and Alder Heniki seconded a motion to adjourn the finance portion of the joint session. The motion carried unanimously on a voice vote and the Finance Committee stood adjourned.

### HR Committee Minutes — August 10th
Alder Wowski moved and Alder Laren seconded approval of the August 10th HR Committee minutes. The motion carried on a voice vote with no opposition.

### ATU Local 1168 Union Grievance — Employee Termination (Closed Session)
A motion was made to adjourn to closed session pursuant to Wisconsin State Statute 19.85(1)(c) and (f) to consider the ATU Local 1168 union grievance regarding an employee termination. The motion required a roll call vote; Alders Mertin, Kilian, Wowski, and Laren all voted aye, and the chair voted in favor as well. The committee proceeded to closed session, with a note from staff that the committee would be required to return to open session before adjourning if any action needed to be taken.

## Public Comment
One speaker addressed the joint committee during the benefit design discussion: Alan Wussellowski, identified as the city engineer residing on the third floor of city hall with 26 years of service, spoke against the scale of the deductible increase and urged the committees to consider increasing the city's HSA contribution to offset the burden on employees and to preserve the city's ability to attract and retain workers. No public comment was offered during the HR-only portion of the meeting.

## Action Items
- 2027 benefit design for City of Wausau and CCITC employees approved by both Finance and HR Committees as presented
- Finance Committee adjourned from joint session
- HR Committee approved August 10th meeting minutes
- HR Committee moved to closed session to consider ATU Local 1168 grievance regarding employee termination; committee to return to open session before final adjournment
