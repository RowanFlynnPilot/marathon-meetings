## Meeting Overview
The Weston Parks Committee met on Monday, November 23, 2026 and voted to set the concession stand as the next fundraising goal for Kennedy Park's Friends group. The committee reviewed end-of-season survey results from the Kennedy Park farmers market, finding mostly positive feedback with parking and traffic flow as the top complaints. Staff described ongoing pool repairs, including filling voids under the deck, with a change order expected at the next meeting. The committee agreed to cancel the December 28, 2026 meeting and hold the next meeting on January 25, 2027.

## Key Discussions
### Pool repair update
Staff reported that contractors filled voids under the pool deck with sandfill and added wire mesh and rebar. Contractors are not expected back for one to two weeks due to missing pump and motor parts. A change order to cover void filling will come to the committee at the next meeting. Staff said funds borrowed for pool repairs should be sufficient to cover costs. One member asked what would happen without filling the voids; staff said the deck could collapse.

### Farmers market end-of-season survey results
Staff presented survey results collected from roughly October 1 through October 31. Most responses were positive on park atmosphere, vendor variety, and food options. Parking and traffic flow were the most common complaints. Staff noted that roundabout construction near the site likely made access more confusing in the first year. The committee discussed improving the parking lot entrance to align with the Kennedy Park master plan, and staff confirmed the farmers market will stay at its current location next year.

### Kennedy Park master plan and next fundraising goal
Staff explained that the village board had not formally set a fundraising goal for the Friends of Kennedy Park group. The committee discussed options including a concession stand, a second large baseball field, and parking lot improvements. Members noted the concession stand could cost around $800,000 based on a master plan estimate, though no design exists yet. One member moved to set the concession stand as the next fundraising goal for the Friends group. The committee approved the motion unanimously.

### Possible next meeting date
Staff proposed canceling the December 28, 2026 meeting because it falls three days after Christmas. The committee agreed to hold the next meeting on January 25, 2027. Staff noted that if urgent action were needed before then, the item could go directly to the full village board.
