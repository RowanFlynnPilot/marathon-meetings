## Key Discussions
### Building Sale and Real Estate Broker Direction
Following a closed session, a supervisor made a motion to direct staff to obtain a real estate broker for purposes of selling a county building, with the condition that any sale must return to the committee for approval and that all current proposals be rejected as submitted. The motion was seconded by Supervisor Leur and passed. It was noted this was a joint motion of both committees meeting together.

### 2027 Annual Budget Overview
County Administrator Leonard briefly addressed the budget, noting an upcoming HR, Finance and Property meeting on Monday to review potential budget amendments. He reminded members that the county is statutorily required to deliver a balanced budget and offered to meet with any supervisor considering amendments before the Friday morning deadline.

### UW Extension Monthly Report and Contract Update
Jen McN (last name partially inaudible) reported that 4-H enrollment is nearly back to prior-year levels, highlighted a 4-H art gallery exhibit at the UW Center for Civic Engagement during National 4-H Month, and noted that two 4-H educators led 40 young adults on a civic engagement trip to Washington DC. She also presented the annual contract for services between Marathon County and UW-Madison Division of Extension for 2027 for informational purposes, noting the contract language is unchanged from prior years with updated service rates already reflected in the proposed budget.

### UWSP-Wausau Monthly Report
The UWSP-Wausau representative reported that the university is on track to be fully out of its former campus by end of next month, with surplus items going to nonprofits and community members. She noted students are settled in at their new NTC space and that starting fall 2027, students will enroll in bachelor's degree programs rather than associate degrees, with applications already open and positive early feedback. A committee member relayed a request from a local historical society member for photographs of the former campus facility to be preserved; the UWSP representative confirmed they have exterior photos and historical interior photos they can share.

### September 3rd, 2026 EED Committee Meeting Minutes Approval
A motion was made by Foot and seconded by Hagen to approve the September 3rd, 2026 meeting minutes. No discussion was held and the motion passed on a voice vote.
