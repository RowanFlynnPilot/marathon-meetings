## Meeting Overview
The Village of Kronenwetter Police and Fire Commission voted unanimously to advance firefighter/first responder applicant Kristopher Grod in the hiring process following a closed-session interview. The brief meeting, held September 28, 2026, was presided over by Vice President Rick Smith in the absence of President Alex Vedvik. All four votes taken at the meeting carried 4-0.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of August 24, 2026 Meeting Minutes
Commissioner Rucinski moved, seconded by Commissioner Rule, to approve the August 24, 2026 meeting minutes as written. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote. No discussion was recorded.

### Closed Session – Interview Fire Department Applicant
Commissioner Rule moved, seconded by Commissioner Udulutch, to convene into closed session pursuant to Wis. Stat. 19.85(1)(c) to interview a Fire Department applicant. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote. The commission entered closed session at 5:01 p.m. and reconvened in open session at 5:37 p.m.

### Action After Closed Session – Firefighter/First Responder Hiring Process
Following the closed-session interview, Commissioner Rule moved, seconded by Commissioner Rucinski, to continue Kristopher Grod forward in the firefighter/first responder hiring process. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.
