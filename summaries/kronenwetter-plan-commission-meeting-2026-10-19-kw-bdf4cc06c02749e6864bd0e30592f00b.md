## Meeting Overview
The Kronenwetter Plan Commission held its October 19, 2026 meeting with only four of seven members present — a bare quorum. The commission approved a zoning change and certified survey map for a residential parcel split on Aspen Road, but voted to postpone action on a controversial conditional use permit for a non-metallic mine at 1320 Kowalski Road, citing the reduced commission membership and the significance of the decision.

## Key Discussions
### Public Hearing: Conditional Use Permit — Eau Claire River LLC, Non-Metallic Mine at 1320 Kowalski Road
Multiple speakers addressed the commission. Guy Fridell, a longtime advocate for a Kowalski Road interchange, strongly opposed the permit, arguing it would undermine decades of village planning and investment, conflict with the comprehensive plan, and that the economic benefits of the interchange — 1,000–1,200 jobs and $100–125 million in new tax base — far outweigh speculative benefits from sand mining. Tom Redens, representing applicant Eau Claire River LLC with Gary Gert and Kurt Sebert, argued the mine would allow the flood-prone site to be graded to a buildable pad and that the village board had already voted months earlier to abandon the interchange committee, making the mine plan the viable path forward for TID-1 development. Neighbors Rich Barker, Dawn Barker (who raised concerns about fish — including walleye — in the existing pond), Dale Champagne, and others also spoke. A public commenter questioned the source of the projected 1,200 jobs and flood storage development challenges.

### New Business: Zoning Change Request — Gene Pachinsky, 3115 Aspen Road (RR5 to RR2)
Community Development Director Nathan Sandwick explained the request: Pachinsky wants to split an 11-acre parcel so the 2.89-acre portion containing the existing home can be sold, but that parcel is too small for RR5 zoning. The commission discussed road frontage (118 feet, deemed sufficient) and the dead-end, low-traffic nature of Aspen Road. Trustee Stowell moved to recommend approval to the village board; Terry Lewis-Burkett seconded. The motion carried 4-0 on a roll call vote.

### New Business: Certified Survey Map — Gene Pachinsky, 3115 Aspen Road
The commission considered approval of the CSM contingent on the village board enacting the zoning change to RR2. Sandwick clarified the plan commission — not the village board — approves CSMs of this type. Trustee Stowell moved approval contingent on the zoning change; Terry Lewis-Burkett seconded. The motion carried 4-0 on roll call.

### New Business: Conditional Use Permit — Eau Claire River LLC, 1320 Kowalski Road
After the public hearing, commissioners expressed reluctance to act with only four of seven members present on such a significant matter. Rick Grundman moved to postpone action; Trustee Stowell seconded. Commissioners noted a 60-day window from the public hearing to render a decision and agreed a special meeting would be scheduled. The postponement motion carried 4-0. Commissioners encouraged absent members to listen to the recorded public hearing testimony before the next meeting.

### Community Development Director Report
Nathan Sandwick highlighted the recent zoning workshop, upcoming Green Tree Trail subdivision final plat review, a potential public hearing on private sewage disposal system ordinance changes, and his attendance at the Wisconsin APA conference. He also flagged a new state law requiring more specificity in comprehensive plans regarding residential growth areas, with compliance required before January 2028 — recommending the village update its plan before that deadline.

### Zoning Code Review
The commission briefly discussed the zoning code review but deferred substantive discussion to the November 16th regular meeting due to the late hour and small attendance. Sandwick noted new state legislation allowing residential TIDs with smaller maximum lot sizes, which could require zoning code amendments if the village wishes to participate. A commissioner requested that someone with expertise on flood storage districts present at a future meeting to help commissioners better understand how flood storage, fill requirements, and development interact.

## Public Comment
No general public comment was offered during the public comment period. However, multiple speakers testified during the public hearing on the Eau Claire River LLC conditional use permit: Guy Fridell (2240 Ruby Drive) opposed the permit at length, citing interchange planning history and economic impact studies; Tom Redens of Land Planning Services spoke on behalf of the applicant; Rich Barker (1290 Kowalski Road) spoke in opposition (brief); Dale Champagne (1292 Kowalski Road) asked a clarifying question about the acreage to be mined; Dawn Barker (1290 Kowalski Road) raised a concern about fish, including walleye, currently in the existing pond; and an unidentified speaker asked where the projected 1,200 jobs would come from. No speakers addressed the Pachinsky zoning change public hearing.
