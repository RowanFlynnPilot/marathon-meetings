      "item": "Public Comment — Flock License Plate Reader System",
      "body": "Felix Gallow urged the committee to pause further funding of the Flock license plate reader system, citing serious data privacy and civil liberties concerns. He stated that 30 days of location data on approximately 450,000 people is accessible to over 1,000 entities across multiple states, that ICE has conducted hundreds or thousands of proxy searches through the system, and that officers have misused it to stalk individuals and surveil people suspected of having abortions. He called the current implementation a violation of the Fourth Amendment and recommended that Marathon County reduce data retention from 30 to 7 days, limit external data sharing, implement rigorous audits, and report results to the public safety committee, directing attendees to unflockwausau.org/marathon for more information."
