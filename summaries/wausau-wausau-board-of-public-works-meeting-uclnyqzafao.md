## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works met on November 10th, 2026, approving a series of project awards and pay estimates totaling over $1.6 million across active city construction and infrastructure projects. Key actions included awarding a trail connector project to PGA, Inc. for approximately $967,000 and approving a public safety roof replacement contract. The board also moved into closed session to consider pre-qualification statements for demolition of 207 North 1st Avenue.

## Key Discussions
### Minutes Approval - November 4, 2026
The minutes from the previous regular Board of Public Works meeting were moved, seconded, and approved without discussion. No dissent was noted.

### Item 2A: Business Campus Trail East-West Connector Award
Staff reported that bids opened November 4th were reviewed for irregularities and submitted to the Wisconsin DOT for authorization to award, which was approved. A motion was made to award the project to PGA, Inc. in the total amount of $967,994.69. The motion was seconded and approved unanimously.

### Item 2B: Construction Administration for Business Campus Trail East-West Connector
Two proposals were received — one from Becker Hoppy and one from MSA Professionals. Staff indicated the proposals were graded by city engineer Alan Wizowski and engineering technician Kurt [Dbor], with MSA Professionals receiving the higher cumulative grade. A motion was made to award the contract to MSA Professionals with the contract amount to be negotiated between engineering and MSA; the motion was seconded and approved unanimously.

A pay estimate for work completed to date was reviewed and approved by Brian Pettit and the presenter. The board approved payment in the total amount of $147,6[inaudible — full figure unclear] unanimously.

### Item 2E: 2026 Street Construction Project A (Stark Street) - Pay Estimate No. 8
Pay estimate for Switlick & Sons for work completed to date, reviewed by Curt Summer and the presenter. The board approved payment of $53,517.61 unanimously without discussion.

### Item 2F: 2026 Street Construction Project B (North 8th Avenue) - Pay Estimate No. 5
Pay estimate for Switlick & Sons reviewed by project inspector Kurt [Dbor] and the presenter in the amount of $432,877.42. The board approved payment unanimously without discussion.

The board voted to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin State Statute 19.85(1)(e) to consider and deliberate on pre-qualification statements for the 2026 demolition of 207 North 1st Avenue, identified as MBX South. The motion was seconded and approved.
