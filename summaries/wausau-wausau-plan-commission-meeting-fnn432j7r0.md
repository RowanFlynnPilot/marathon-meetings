## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Plan Commission met on October 20, 2026 and approved five action items, including the landmark designation of Yawkey Park, a rezoning at 909 South 60th Avenue from medium to heavy industrial, a preliminary plat for the West Hill First Addition subdivision, and two alley/road vacations. The Yawkey Park landmarking, supported by public comment from the Yawkey family heirs and a unanimous Historic Preservation Commission vote, will advance to City Council for final approval.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comment – Yawkey Park Landmarking
Two speakers addressed the commission. Kathy Foley, 1209 East Hill Drive, read a statement on behalf of the Yawkey family heirs — great-grandchildren Alice Woodson Smith, A. Woodson Haigie, Abigail Spire, Lee Tuckey, and Nancy Lee Fischer — expressing full support for designating Yawkey Park as a historic landmark and calling for protection of its 106-year-old footprint 'deeded for use by the public in perpetuity.' Christine Martins, 1228 Arthur Street, noted she authored the nomination document and that the Historic Preservation Commission had already passed it unanimously with no opposition at the public hearing stage.

### Consideration of Minutes – September 15, 2026
A motion to approve the September 15, 2026 minutes was made by Bruggeman and seconded by Balkin. No corrections were offered and the minutes were approved by voice vote.

### Potential Landmarking of Yawkey Park (Item E, moved up)
The mayor moved this item up on the agenda to accommodate attending citizens. A motion to approve was made by Bruggeman and seconded by Balkin with no discussion. The commission approved the landmarking unanimously by voice vote; the mayor noted the measure would proceed to City Council for final action.

### Rezoning of 909 South 60th Avenue – Medium Industrial to Heavy Industrial (Item 4A)
Assistant City Planner Edmonson explained that the parcel is surrounded by heavy industrial uses and natural features that buffer it from other development, and noted that a neighboring parcel to the north had recently received a similar rezoning. Staff found no concerns with the application and recommended approval as consistent with surrounding properties. The commission approved the rezoning unanimously by voice vote; the mayor confirmed it would go to November City Council.

### West Hill First Addition Preliminary Plat (Item 4B)
Planner Lenz explained the preliminary plat covers a new subdivision west of Stettin Elementary School, with Arborvitae Drive designed to connect to the adjacent Greentree subdivision to the west. The INF Committee had previously recommended approval unanimously. The commission approved the preliminary plat unanimously; the developer will return with a final plat for the phase-one lots.

### Vacating and Discontinuing Alley Bounded by Lakeview Drive, Bertha Street, Elmwood Boulevard, and Midway Boulevard (Item 4C)
The mayor described the alley as an unpaved grass strip with limited use and no garage access dependency. All adjacent property owners except 2514 signed a petition to vacate, and a utility easement would remain in place. The INF Committee had previously recommended approval. The commission approved the vacation unanimously by voice vote.

### Vacating and Discontinuing 134-ft Southerly Extension of Liberty Ridgeway (Item 4D)
Commissioner Trueblood explained that Liberty Way continues to the east and north, making this southern stub an undeveloped dead end; a large home is being constructed in the area, making vacation appropriate. He stated he 'strongly advised' approval. A utility easement will remain. The commission approved the vacation unanimously by voice vote.

## Public Comment
Two speakers offered public comment, both regarding Yawkey Park landmarking. Kathy Foley (1209 East Hill Drive) read a written statement from five Yawkey family heirs supporting the landmark designation to protect the park's 106-year-old footprint for public use in perpetuity. Christine Martins (1228 Arthur Street), who authored the nomination, noted the Historic Preservation Commission had passed it unanimously and that no opposition had been raised at any prior public hearing.

## Action Items
- Yawkey Park landmark designation forwarded to Wausau City Council for final approval
- Rezoning of 909 South 60th Avenue from medium to heavy industrial forwarded to November City Council
- West Hill First Addition Preliminary Plat approved; developer to return with final plat for phase-one lots
- Alley vacation (Lakeview/Bertha/Elmwood/Midway) approved, utility easement to remain
- Liberty Ridgeway 134-ft southerly stub vacation approved, utility easement to remain
- Next regular Plan Commission meeting scheduled for November 17, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.
