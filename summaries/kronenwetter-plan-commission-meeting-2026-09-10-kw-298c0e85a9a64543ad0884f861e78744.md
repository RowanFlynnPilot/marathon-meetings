## Meeting Overview
Based on the published agenda, the Kronenwetter Plan Commission was scheduled to hold a public hearing and consider three significant land development matters: a conditional use permit and site plan for a Marathon County Highway Facility, a zoning change request for 26.7 acres on Lea Road from agricultural to rural residential use, and a text amendment to village zoning ordinances to permit zero-lot-line homes.

## Key Discussions
### Conditional Use Permit - Marathon County Facility
Troy Torgerson was scheduled to request a Conditional Use Permit for a Highway Facility on 25.2470 acres at 2384 & 2388 Old Highway 51, Kronenwetter. The Plan Commission was set to review this application during both the public hearing and new business portions of the meeting.

### Site Plan - Marathon County Facility
The Plan Commission was scheduled to consider and possibly act on the site plan submission for the proposed Marathon County Highway Facility at the same Kronenwetter location. This review was expected to accompany the conditional use permit discussion.

### Zoning Change - Cywinski 1900 & 1904 Lea Road
William Cywinski was scheduled to request a zoning change of 26.7 acres from AR (Agriculture and Residential) to RR5 (Rural Residential 5) at 1904 Lea Road, Kronenwetter. The Plan Commission was set to review this application during both the public hearing and new business sections of the meeting.

### CSM Cywinski - Land Division at 1900 & 1904 Lea Road
William Cywinski was scheduled to request a Certified Survey Map division of land totaling 33 acres across two parcels to create two new lots and one out lot. The Plan Commission was expected to consider this land division proposal along with the related zoning change request.

### Text Amendment of Chapter 520 - Zero Lot Line Homes
The Village of Kronenwetter was scheduled to present proposed amendments to the Zoning ordinance (Chapter 520) to allow Zero-Lot-Line Residences. The amendments were expected to modify subsections 520-20 (Residential land use types) and 520-138 (Definitions), with a draft ordinance available for public review at the Municipal Center.
