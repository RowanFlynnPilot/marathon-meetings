## Meeting Overview
The Mosinee School Board approved a resolution requesting an early start date for the 2027-2028 school year and accepted the resignation of the Director of Finance at its November 17, 2026 meeting. The board also approved the district's Technology Usage Guide and scheduled a special meeting for December 1, 2026, to address policy matters including classroom displays. All substantive votes carried 9-0.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of Agenda
The board voted to move two closed-session items — Board Members and Chain of Command, and Board Members and Conflict of Interest — into open session pursuant to Wis. Stat. § 19.85(1)(f). David Bauer moved and Derek Nest seconded; the motion carried 9-0. The full agenda with updates was then approved on a motion by Diane Gorman, seconded by Donna Kaiser, carrying 9-0.

### Special Presentation: Special Education Update
Director of Student Services/Special Education Deidre Yirkovsky presented the board with an update on the Special Education program. No vote was taken on this item.

### Special Presentation: Act 20 Presentation
Director of Innovation and Strategic Growth Tom McCarty presented an update on the district's progress with Act 20. Kathy Butalla was also present to assist in answering board questions. No vote was taken.

### Special Presentation: State Report Cards
Tom McCarty discussed how state report card data is collected and what information is presented on the report card for the 2025-26 school year. No vote was taken.

### Special Presentation: Local Report Cards
Middle School Principal Kate Wollersheim presented the middle school local report card and High School Principal Mia Chmiel presented the high school local report card for the 2025-26 school year. No vote was taken.

### Consent Agenda
The consent agenda, encompassing the Finance & Building Committee report, Personnel Committee report, and Instructional Programs Committee report, was approved on a motion by Diane Gorman, seconded by Donna Kaiser, carrying 9-0.

### Approval of Monthly Voucher List
The board approved the monthly voucher list on a motion by Derek Nest, seconded by Diane Gorman. All nine directors voted aye; the motion carried 9-0.

### Acceptance of Monthly Treasurer's Report
The board approved the monthly treasurer's report on a motion by Derek Nest, seconded by Diane Gorman. All nine directors voted aye; the motion carried 9-0.

### New Business: Special School Board Meeting
The board voted to schedule a special meeting on December 1, 2026, with a discipline meeting at 5:30 PM and the special meeting at 7:00 PM, to allow more in-depth discussion of pending agenda items. Joe Knoedler moved and Diane Gorman seconded; the motion carried 9-0.

### New Business: Board Agenda Planning — Policy 2240.01 and Tech Usage Guide Tabling
The board voted to table agenda items on Policy 2240.01 (Classroom Displays) and the Tech Usage Guide to the December 1 special meeting for decisions, on a motion by Diane Gorman, seconded by Megan Stankowski, carrying 9-0. However, the Tech Usage Guide was subsequently acted upon: Calandra Zebro moved and David Bauer seconded a motion to approve the Tech Usage Guide with the addition of it as a recurring agenda item for review throughout the year, which carried 9-0.

### New Business: Resolution to Request Early Start Date for 2027-2028 School Year
No community members spoke during the public comment period on the resolution. The board then approved the resolution requesting an early start date for the 2027-2028 school year, with an amendment updating the document to reference the Mosinee School District by name. Megan Stankowski moved and Donna Kaiser seconded; the motion carried 9-0.

### New Business: Board Members and Chain of Command
President Paul Nievinski advised board members to avoid making promises to community members and to refer constituents to the proper chain of command. Diane Gorman reiterated that the board's position is to remain neutral. Jessica Maple emphasized that members with children in the district should remember they represent all students, not just their own. No vote was taken.

### New Business: Board Members and Conflict of Interest
President Nievinski provided examples of conflicts of interest for board members. Jessica Maple reminded members that if they intend to visit district buildings, they should notify the superintendent or building administrator in advance. No vote was taken.

### Return to Open Session: Resignation of Director of Finance
Upon returning from closed session, the board approved the resignation of the Director of Finance on a motion by Diane Gorman, seconded by Calandra Zebro, carrying 9-0. The board also approved the closed session minutes on a motion by Diane Gorman, seconded by Calandra Zebro, carrying 9-0.

## Public Comment
One resident spoke during the public forum, addressing concerns about the Green Acres bus route.
