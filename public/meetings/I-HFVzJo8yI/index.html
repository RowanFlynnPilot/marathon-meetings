Approval of August 25, 2026 Minutes

A motion was made by Watson and seconded by Neil to approve the minutes of the previous meeting. No discussion was noted and the motion carried.

Drinking Water Treatment Solar Array Project — Budget Modification and Sole Source Engineering Request

Staff reported that the city did not receive the anticipated EPA grant, which would have significantly offset construction costs for the approximately $2.8 million solar array project. Without the grant, the payback period was estimated at roughly 21 years even with the 30% federal tax credit, which committee members characterized as too long to be financially viable. Alder Watson expressed disappointment but acknowledged the math was unfavorable, and noted that community engagement and 30% design work completed could be preserved for future efforts. The chair noted the city may have 'missed the window' for favorable incentives. A motion to approve moving forward was made and seconded, but defeated 1-4 with members voting no in anticipation of rejecting the project while expressing hope it would not be abandoned permanently.

Sole Source Request — Lucas Mechanical CPR Devices from Stryker for Fire Department

The fire chief explained the department is switching back to Lucas devices from a previous brand, with existing devices to be traded in for an $18,000 credit. Alder Hani identified a approximately $750 math discrepancy in the sole source document between the stated total cost and the calculated cost after the trade-in, and also flagged what appeared to be a copy-paste reference to a video laryngoscope in the document. The chief acknowledged the error, attributed it to the EMS division chief who prepared the paperwork and was unavailable, and committed to providing corrected figures. The committee approved the purchase pending correction of the math error and typo, with the corrected net cost calculated as $72,692.92.
