Discussion

Approval of October 6, 2026 Meeting Minutes

A motion was made by a commissioner identified as Galhar and seconded by Van Craig. The minutes were approved unanimously with no changes noted.

2027 Lead Service Line Funding Award from DNR

Director Lynman reported that the city received less DNR funding than anticipated and budgeted for. He explained that the absence of a lead service line replacement ordinance hurt the city's score on a second project, costing approximately $2 million in principal forgiveness. That $2 million will now need to be borrowed as additional debt. Lynman noted the matter will go to the finance committee on November 10th, coinciding with the full council's budget consideration, and may result in a budget amendment for additional borrowing. The chair noted that the alternative is to 'do less with what we've got.'

Wastewater Project Updates: Headworks, Crocker Street Lift Station, Sewer Rehab

Staff member Ben provided updates: the Headworks project is nearing completion with a final completion date of November 15th; the Crocker Street lift station is well underway with the site closed up and asphalt repaved; the Cherry Street lift station project is nearly closed out; and the 2026 sewer rehab project was awarded to Visions, though a project kickoff meeting has not yet been scheduled. No votes were taken on these updates.

Item 3A: Temporary Construction Easements for Parcels 1, 2, 3, and 6

Staff explained the city holds easements over utility infrastructure outside the roadway right-of-way, and the DOT is requesting a temporary construction easement over those existing easements for grading work during the road project. No utility relocation is required. A commissioner identified as Handley questioned why no reimbursement was being sought; staff clarified the city owns no property being given up and will incur no costs, so reimbursement is not applicable. The motion was made by Martins and seconded by Hadley, and the item passed unanimously.

Item 3B: Quit Claim Deed for Parcel 6

Staff explained that on Parcel 6, the DOT is acquiring a permanent limited easement from the property owner for drainage purposes and is asking the city to relinquish any rights it may hold there via a quit claim deed. Staff confirmed the city has no utilities in that area and that the city attorney's office and city engineering have both signed off. Motion was made by Martins, seconded by Hadley, and passed unanimously.

Item 3C: Statement of Non-Reimbursement by Utility

The commission considered a formal statement confirming the utility will not seek reimbursement from the DOT for the transportation project, consistent with the earlier discussion that no utility relocation costs will be incurred. Motion was moved by one commissioner and seconded by Galhar. The item passed unanimously with no further debate.
