Approval of September 30th, 2026 Meeting Minutes

A motion to approve the previous meeting minutes was made by Supervisor Cavlli and seconded by Supervisor Kremes. The chair nearly skipped the vote before catching himself. The motion carried on a voice vote.

2027 Annual Budget Overview

Supervisor Robinson presented the budget overview, noting the approximately $235 million budget represents a significant increase over the prior year largely due to inclusion of the highway facility. He described it as a 'status quo budget' and highlighted that net new construction growth of only 1.5% necessitated fee increases. Robinson also flagged a newly announced DNR PFAS community sampling grant with a 20% match requirement that the health department is considering, with the village of Maine identified as an area of particular concern due to impacted wells. Lance, the county administrator, asked committee members to contact his office before Friday at 8:00 a.m. if they wished to propose amendments for the finance committee's Monday meeting. No amendments were proposed by Health and Human Services.

CART and Mobile Crisis Services Expansion

Committee member Katie raised questions about the status of discussions to expand CART (Crisis Assessment Response Team). Lance reported that North Central Healthcare executive director Jason Hake had inquired about CART expansion but that it was not built into the published budget. He noted modifications are being made to increase availability of North Central Healthcare crisis professionals for community response when law enforcement requests assistance. Supervisor John added that discussions with the sheriff's and police departments have shifted focus from CART expansion specifically toward broader mobile crisis service expansion, with a grant funding evaluation ongoing.

2027 8521 Elderly and Disabled Transportation Grant Application

Staff presented the annual application for 8521 funds supporting elderly and disabled transit services in the county. The county is eligible to receive $366,060 from the state, requiring a local match of $73,201 covered through a partnership with Metroride, North Central Healthcare, and CPZ. Supervisor Ron asked where the local match funds were budgeted, and staff confirmed they are in CPZ's budget included in the 2027 budget before the board. The Transportation Coordinating Committee held a public hearing on October 21st with no public comments received and forwarded the application to this committee. Supervisor Rosenberg moved to approve forwarding the grant application to the county board; Supervisor Foot seconded, and the motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

Action items

  • Forward the 2027 8521 Elderly and Disabled Transportation Grant application to the full county board for approval; application due to the state in December.
  • Committee members wishing to propose budget amendments should contact the county administrator's office by 8:00 a.m. Friday for finance committee consideration; amendments may also be introduced through final budget adoption.
  • Health department to continue evaluating the DNR PFAS community sampling grant opportunity, particularly for the village of Maine.
  • Law enforcement and health partners to continue evaluation of grant funding for expanded mobile crisis services.
  • Next committee meeting: Wednesday, December 2nd, 2026 at 3:00 p.m.
