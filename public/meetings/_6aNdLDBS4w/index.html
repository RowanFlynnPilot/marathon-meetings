Discussion

Ordinance 26-013: Nuisance Abatement Property Storage and Disposal

The Board was scheduled to consider an ordinance amending Section 50.103 of the Municipal Code to establish procedures for the storage and disposal of property removed during nuisance abatement efforts. This measure was set to provide clear regulatory guidelines for handling materials seized during municipal code enforcement actions.

Ordinance 26-014: Animal Fanciers Code Amendment

The Board was scheduled to consider an ordinance amending Section 10.123 regarding animal fanciers in the Village's Code of Ordinances. This agenda item was set to address regulatory changes related to the keeping of animals in the municipality.

Joint Resolution 2026-001: Police Headquarters Debt Authorization

The Board was scheduled to consider a joint resolution with the Village of Rothschild and Mountain Bay Metropolitan Police Department authorizing debt for construction of a new police department headquarters and approving an intergovernmental agreement for sharing costs and expenses between the two villages. This significant capital project was set for discussion regarding financing mechanisms and cost-sharing arrangements.

Resolution 2026-021: Stormwater Coalition Cooperative Agreement

The Board was scheduled to consider a resolution approving a cooperative agreement with the North Central Wisconsin Stormwater Coalition. This measure was set to establish or continue the Village's participation in regional stormwater management and environmental protection efforts.

Resolution 2026-022: Bayberry Street and Concord Avenue Reconstruction

The Board was scheduled to consider an authorizing resolution for the reconstruction of Bayberry Street and Concord Avenue. This project was set for action to authorize design, engineering, and construction of roadway improvements in the Village.

Establishing 2027 Budget Priorities

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on establishing budget priorities for the fiscal year 2027. This discussion was set to guide departmental budgeting and allocation of municipal resources for the upcoming year.

Parks and Recreation Committee Recommendation: Farmers Market at Kennedy Park

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on the Parks and Recreation Committee's recommendation to continue operating the farmers market at Kennedy Park. This item was set to determine whether to maintain this recurring community event.

Municipal Center Rooms as Public Meeting/Gathering Spaces Policy

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on the use of Municipal Center rooms as public meeting and gathering spaces, including consideration of an accompanying draft policy. This measure was set to establish guidelines and procedures for community use of Village facilities.

Project #202600263: Rezoning Request for 4205 & 4307 Weston Avenue

The Board was scheduled to consider a rezoning request to change the classification of properties at 4205 and 4307 Weston Avenue from B-2 Highway Business to LI Limited Industrial. The Plan Commission recommended denial of this rezoning application, and the Board was set to make a final determination on the request.

Buchkowski Animal Fancier License at 5512 N. Birch Lane

The Board was scheduled to consider an animal fancier license application for 5512 N. Birch Lane. The Plan Commission recommended denial of this application, and the Board was set to review and make a final determination.

Lamar Perpetual Easement Request at 8702 Progress Way

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on a request from Lamar to purchase a perpetual easement over Village-owned real estate at 8702 Progress Way. This transaction was set for Board consideration regarding terms and conditions of the easement agreement.

President's Appointments to Committees and/or Commissions

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on President Maloney's appointments to various Village committees and commissions. This agenda item was set to fill vacancies and ensure adequate board representation across municipal bodies.

Everest Metro Police Department and Municipal Court Fund Balance Allocation

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on the allocation of fund balance from the Everest Metro Police Department and Municipal Court. This financial matter was set to determine appropriate use of accumulated reserves.

Employee Appreciation and Monthly Staff Recognition

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on employee appreciation initiatives, including consideration of a standing monthly staff recognition agenda item. This measure was set to enhance employee morale and establish formal recognition procedures.

Community Development Authority Role and Responsibilities

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on the Community Development Authority's role and responsibilities going forward. This discussion was set to clarify the CDA's mission and operational direction for future development efforts.

Aquatic Center Operational and Capital Costs and Partnerships

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on the Aquatic Center's operational and capital costs and possible future municipal or community partnerships. This discussion was set to examine financial sustainability and alternative management models for the facility.

Policy for Receipt and Handling of Written Comments at Public Meetings

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on drafting a policy for the receipt and handling of written comments received at public meetings. This procedural measure was set to establish standardized practices for managing public input.

Letter of Support for Greater Wausau Prosperity Partnership Destination JEM Grant

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on a request for a letter of support for the Greater Wausau Prosperity Partnership's Destination JEM Grant application to the Wisconsin Department of Tourism for the Ride Wisconsin Gravel project. The Board was set to determine whether to endorse this regional tourism and economic development initiative.

Final TIF#1 Audit Contract

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on a contract for the final Tax Increment Financing District #1 audit. This agenda item was set to select an auditor and authorize completion of financial review for the TIF district.

Bank Proposals for New Police Headquarters Financing

The Board was scheduled to discuss and take action on bank proposals for financing the Village's portion of the new police headquarters construction. This financial matter was set to evaluate lending options and terms for the capital project.
