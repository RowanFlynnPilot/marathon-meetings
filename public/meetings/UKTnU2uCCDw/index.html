Discussion

Approval of September 22, 2026 Regular Board of Public Works Minutes

A board member moved to approve the minutes and another seconded. The motion passed unanimously with no discussion.

Recommendation on 2026 Parking Lot Lighting Proposals

Staff reported that proposals were opened on September 26 and only one bid was received from Lighting Design Solutions at $14,447 per twin-head pole unit. A staff member identified as Rick confirmed the amount. The board moved to approve the sole bid, which passed unanimously.

Payment Estimate #34 – Community Infrastructure Partners – Lead Service Line Replacement

Staff presented Payment Estimate #34 from Community Infrastructure Partners covering a mix of engineering, construction, and other costs totaling $2,115,477.27 for the lead service line replacement program. The board moved to approve and the motion passed unanimously without debate.

Pay Estimate – Community Infrastructure Partners – Outreach Grant Program (August Work)

Staff described this as a separate pot of money covering outreach costs for work completed in August, totaling $24,882.13. The board approved the payment unanimously with no discussion.

Pay Estimate #11 – 2025 Street Construction Project A, Randolph Street (Hos Suns Incorporated)

Staff explained this is the final pay estimate for all work completed on the Randolph Street project from Cherry Street, with retainage being reduced to $10,000 pending spring restoration verification. The total payment recommended was $160,458.60. The board approved unanimously.

Pay Estimate #1 – 2026 Sidewalk Repair Project B (Pember Companies)

Staff noted this is expected to be the one and only pay estimate for the project until final retainage release, with retainage reduced to $1,000 and a total pay estimate of $97,721. The board approved unanimously with no discussion.
