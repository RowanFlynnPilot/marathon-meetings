Human Resources, Finance & Property Committee Meeting

October 12, 2026 · 6m
BudgetTax Levy2027 County BudgetMill RatePublic Hearing

The Marathon County Human Resources, Finance & Property Committee held a brief meeting focused on authorizing publication of the proposed 2027 county budget. The committee unanimously approved publication of the budget notice, which includes a proposed tax levy of $66,248,545 and a mill rate of 3.4773, advancing the budget process toward a public hearing on November 2nd and final county board action on November 10th.

Discussion

Consideration of Amendments to the 2027 Budget and Authorization of Publication

County Administrator and Finance Director Brian Fensky reported that no proposed amendments to the budget had been received since the previous meeting. Fensky provided a high-level overview of the publication notice, which includes actual 2025 figures, amended and estimated 2026 figures, and the proposed 2027 budget broken down by category; he noted that state special charges were included in the general fund this year as a new addition. Chair Gibbs asked Fensky to clarify a significant increase in 'other financing uses' under expenditures, which Fensky explained represents transfers to other funds, including $5,884,464 in transfers proposed for 2027. The committee then approved publication unanimously, with the public hearing set for November 2nd and final county board action on November 10th.
