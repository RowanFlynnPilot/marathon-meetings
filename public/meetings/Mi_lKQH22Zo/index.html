Discussion

Various License Applications (excluding Fuel On)

The committee moved to approve all license applications as presented with the exception of the Fuel On location, which was taken up separately. Alder True Blood moved, Alder Watson seconded, and the motion passed unanimously.

Fuel On (Sunny Express) Provisional Liquor License and Tobacco License

City Clerk Cody presented a timeline showing the business was sold on September 23, 2026, and operated without a license for approximately 29 days before the city was notified on October 5. The new owner applied for a license on October 21 and had been in compliance since October 5. Captain Melinda Pauls confirmed the business had been compliant with checks for both alcohol and tobacco over the prior two months. Discussion arose over whether to grant the tobacco license alongside the provisional liquor license or wait until the 60-day provisional period ended; Alder Solonsky raised concerns about whether tobacco could be revisited if the provisional liquor license was not converted. Alder Watson moved to approve both the provisional liquor license per the liquor review committee's recommendation and the cigarette/tobacco license; Alder Lukans seconded. The motion passed unanimously. Alder True Blood noted that background information on the compliance gap should have been included in the committee packet.

Formal Expression of Concern: M&R Station, Richard Gordon DBA M&R Station LLC

Richard Gordon appeared to respond to a 100-point demerit assessment for failure to maintain an orderly premises related to a shooting incident. Gordon disputed characterizations of the event, arguing it was a premeditated attack by outsiders targeting a specific victim and that his staff had no way to know a shooting had occurred. Captain Pauls and Deputy Chief Payton testified that the suspect's vehicle had been parked in M&R's lot waiting, that both suspect and victim had been inside M&R together, and that a named suspect stated a staff member announced a 'BB gun' had been fired — indicating staff may have been aware of the incident without notifying police. Alder Lukans noted the demerit system functions as a strict liability standard for maintaining orderly premises. The committee was also informed by property manager Stacy Holt and attorney Karina Fischer that the property owners have issued a 28-day notice to Gordon and will proceed with eviction if he has not vacated by November 30. City staff clarified there is no formal process for removing assessed demerit points and that at 100 points no automatic action is triggered; revocation at a future hearing would be required to prevent Gordon from holding a license for 12 months. No formal vote or action was taken on this item.

Emergency Management Service Agreement with Marathon County

Alder True Blood moved to approve the emergency management service agreement with Marathon County as submitted; Alder Solonsky seconded. The motion passed unanimously with no debate.

Wausau Fire Department Quarter 3 2026 Report

The fire chief highlighted that the apprenticeship program formally launched September 28, with nine apprentices beginning an 18-month program covering EMT basic, Firefighter 1, Firefighter 2, and paramedic credentials. The department currently has six vacancies; three qualified firefighter-paramedics will start in December, reducing the gap to three. The apprenticeship program is intended to address future vacancies on an ongoing basis.

Homelessness Framework and Strategic Plan Update

Marathon County Public Health Coordinator Tracy Duranti's colleague presented an update on the foundational framework, noting a steering committee has been formed with city and county representation including Tracy Duranti from the police department and Randy Fifick from community development. Since August, over 120 unduplicated intake surveys of people experiencing homelessness have been collected through partner agencies. A daylong strategic planning session with over 30 community partners was held to inform the plan. The strategic plan draft will be presented to the committee in December for feedback, with a formal launch and joint city-county vote scheduled for January 26 at the civic engagement center.
