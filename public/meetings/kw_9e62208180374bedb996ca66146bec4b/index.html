Special Village Board Meeting

The Kronenwetter Village Board held a special meeting on August 27, 2026, with one primary action item: accepting the resignation of Village Clerk Jennifer Poyer, which passed 6-0. The meeting was preceded by pointed public comment from three residents criticizing the board over two costly workplace investigations totaling $105,000, vague closed session notices, and broader governance concerns. Trustee Stowell was absent.

Discussion

Public Comment

Three residents spoke critically of the village board. Bruny Kramer of 2150 E. State Hwy 153 criticized a pattern of poor governance over the past six months, referencing a mining situation and excessive closed sessions, and suggested board members seek training. Chris Johnson of 1998 Woodgate Lane delivered an extended rebuke, alleging that a workplace investigation costing approximately $40,000 was rooted in ego between President Joling and former administrator Sorenson, that closed session notices were improperly vague, and that employee privacy was mishandled through inadequate redactions; Johnson also addressed an apparent comment made about her Facebook posts before the meeting, calling it unprofessional. Guy Fredl of 2240 Ruby Drive, identifying himself as a longtime village volunteer, presented specific cost figures—$60,000 for a first investigation by Von Briesen and Roper and $45,000 for a second, totaling $105,000—and noted the village had lost three employees and three female board members in recent years, warning of potential litigation from Peter Wagner and Jennifer Poyer and suggesting the village hire a contracted HR professional to handle such matters.

Acceptance of Clerk's Resignation

The board voted 6-0 to accept the resignation of Village Clerk Jennifer Poyer as submitted. Following the vote, a trustee—identified in the transcript as raising additional comments—asked that staff coordinate a transition plan covering return of village property, system and social media passwords, and a status report from Poyer on outstanding work items. The same trustee thanked Poyer for her work on community events, social media, and elections, and wished her well. President Joling noted the village may need to call an additional meeting if an interim clerk is secured.

Action items

  • Staff to develop a coordinated transition plan for the clerk position, including return of village property and transfer of system, program, and social media passwords
  • Outgoing clerk Jennifer Poyer requested (though not required) to provide a status report on outstanding work items
  • Board may convene an additional special meeting if an interim clerk arrangement is reached
