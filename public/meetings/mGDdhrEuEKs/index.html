Closed Session Announcement — Proposals and MCAC Lease Direction

Staff announced that, following closed session, committees provided direction on next steps for engaging each of the submitted proposals for potential negotiation. Additionally, staff were directed to begin lease negotiations with the Marathon Center for Arts and Culture (MCAC). A speaker — identified as Chair Marash — emphasized that any resulting agreement must return to the appropriate committees and to the full board for approval before taking effect.

EEED Committee Adjournment

A motion to adjourn the EEED Committee was made by Supervisor Cabell and seconded by Supervisor Foot. The motion passed on a voice vote with no opposition. The HRFC Committee then announced a brief five-minute recess, with a note that an ERC meeting was scheduled to begin at 2:30.
