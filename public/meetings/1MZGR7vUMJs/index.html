Quasi-judicial closed session — affirmation vote

Upon reconvening from closed session, Alder Neil moved to affirm the quasi-judicial decision and the motion was seconded by Alder Trueblood. The committee approved the motion 4-0 with no opposition noted.

County-owned property at 625 N. 28th Street — potential park acquisition

The committee discussed whether to enter closed session regarding possible acquisition of a county-owned property on 28th Street for park development. Alder Sho stated agreement with Alder Neil that the committee had no interest in purchasing the property, citing the conditional cost of rehabilitating it. Alder Slonsky concurred, saying she saw no reason the city would have interest in purchasing the property for park development. With consensus against pursuing the acquisition, no motion was made and the item was passed over without action.
