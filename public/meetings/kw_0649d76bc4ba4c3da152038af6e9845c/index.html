The Village of Kronenwetter Board held its November 9, 2026 meeting primarily to adopt the 2027 operating budget, including all three budget resolutions covering the general fund, all other funds, and the capital improvements program. The board also formally appointed Connie Nowak as village clerk and renewed its legal services agreement with Wolfram, Gamoke and Hutchinson. All items passed unanimously 7-0, with Finance Director John presenting a detailed budget overview highlighting a proposed assessed tax rate of $4.25, a 6.79% tax levy increase, and no water or sewer rate increases for 2027.

Discussion

Public Hearing - 2027 Operating Budget

One member of the public, identified as Bernie, spoke during the public hearing. He commented on the state levy limit calculations, the disparity between Kronenwetter's high residential tax base (84%) versus commercial-heavy communities like Schofield (39%), questioned the need for a deputy clerk given Friday office closures, and expressed strong opposition to the village's ethics committee, calling it 'totally meaningless.' No other members of the public spoke during the hearing.

2027 Budget Presentation

Finance Director John presented a detailed budget overview. Key highlights included a proposed assessed tax rate of $4.25 (equalized equivalent of $2.71), a total tax levy of approximately $2.9 million representing a 6.79% increase, no water or sewer rate increases, a garbage fee increase of $39.24 largely due to a diesel fuel surcharge, and a 3.5% COLA for non-elected positions. John noted the village's unassigned fund balance has grown from 3.52% in 2023 to approximately 34% for 2025, and previewed that a 2027 revaluation will likely drop the assessed tax rate significantly in 2028. Trustee Coyle thanked staff for presenting a 'competent budget,' implicitly referencing past budget shortfalls in 2023-2024 when debt service was underfunded by approximately $1.2 million over two years.

Resolution 2026-013 - Adopt 2027 General Fund Operating Budget

Trustee Lesniak moved and Trustee Stowell seconded adoption of the 2027 general fund operating budget with a total appropriation of $6.5 million and a tax levy increase of 6.79%. The resolution passed unanimously 7-0 on roll call vote with no dissent or notable debate.

Resolution 2026-014 - Adopt 2027 Budget for Debt Service, Special Revenue, Capital Projects, and Proprietary Funds

Trustee Stowell moved and Trustee Mishka seconded adoption of the resolution covering all other funds including debt service, special revenue, capital projects, TID districts, and utility funds. The resolution passed unanimously 7-0 on roll call with no discussion.

Resolution 2026-015 - Adopt 2027 Capital Improvements Program Budget

Trustee Coyle moved and Trustee Solheim seconded adoption of the 2027 CIP budget totaling approximately $4.3 million, with proposed general obligation borrowing of roughly $1.865 million. John clarified that approving the CIP budget does not authorize immediate borrowing; separate borrowing resolutions must come before the board in 2027. The resolution passed unanimously 7-0.

Resolution 2026-016 - Appointment of Connie Nowak as Village Clerk

Trustee Mishka moved and Trustee Mortensen seconded appointing Connie Nowak as village clerk. Finance Director John flagged a potential error in the resolution's first 'whereas' clause, noting the charter ordinance specifies April as the start of the term rather than October; the board agreed to accept an abbreviated appointment now and clean up the language consistent with charter requirements. Multiple trustees praised Nowak, with Joling noting residents had already given her positive feedback. The resolution passed unanimously 7-0.

Legal Services Agreement with Wolfram, Gamoke and Hutchinson

Trustee Mortensen moved and Trustee Solheim seconded approval of the legal services agreement. Brief discussion noted the contract is essentially the same as the existing one and that Village Attorney Wolfram has been well regarded, including by the former police chief. The agreement passed unanimously 7-0.

Future Agenda Items

Village Administrator Jim raised the possibility of moving from two board meetings per month to one meeting per month on the fourth Monday, framing it as a way to streamline committee-to-board workflow and reduce scheduling burdens on trustees. He noted an ordinance change would be required and indicated it would be a discussion item at the next meeting. Discussion also touched on the rationale for Friday afternoon office closures, with staff explaining that uninterrupted time is needed for meeting minutes and audit tasks, and that a deputy clerk hire is planned for late first quarter 2027.
