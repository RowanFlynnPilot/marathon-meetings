Discussion

Conditional Use Permit (Eau Claire River LLC)

Eau Claire River, LLC was scheduled to present a request for a Conditional Use Permit to operate a nonmetallic mine on a 35.58-acre parcel at 1320 Kowalski Rd in Kronenwetter. The commission was set to hold a public hearing on this proposal and expected to consider action on the permit application.

Zoning Change Request (Jean Pochinski)

Jean Pochinski was scheduled to request a zoning change of 2.89 acres at 3115 Aspen Rd from RR5 (Rural Residential 5) to RR2 (Rural Residential 2) zoning classification. The commission was set to hold a public hearing and expected to consider this zoning reclassification request.

Certified Survey Map (Jean Pochinski)

Jean Pochinski was scheduled to request approval of a Certified Survey Map to divide an 11-acre parcel into two lots at 3115 Aspen Rd. The commission was expected to review and possibly act on this land division application.

Community Development Director Report

The Plan Commission was scheduled to receive a report from the Community Development Director regarding ongoing village development matters and planning initiatives.

Zoning Code Review

The commission was set to discuss and review the village's zoning code as part of its ongoing regulatory oversight responsibilities.
