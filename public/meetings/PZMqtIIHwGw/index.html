Discussion

Public Comment - County Road W Safety Concerns

Two Town of Texas residents, Tracy Delaney (236617 County Road W) and Donald Hillbrand (236493 County Road W), spoke about dangerous conditions on County Road W. Delaney requested no-engine-braking signs due to noise from increased truck traffic from Hos, and asked the committee to add guardrails on the east side of County Road W north of Marshall Hill Road when road improvements are planned, noting her house is only 30 feet from the road. Hillbrand echoed concerns about out-of-control speeds, outdated and unsafe guardrails, vehicles crossing into driveways and nearly striking his house, and a semi that refused to yield to an ambulance — for which Sheriff Chad Bilip fined the driver and firm $500 each. Hillbrand noted that a consulting firm had agreed guardrails were needed.

2026 Marathon County Culvert Bridge Aid (Resolution 5726)

Kevin [last name not stated] presented the 2026 Culvert Bridge Aid program, describing it as a fairly typical year with approximately $64,000 in projects, with county crews completing five projects for municipalities. A larger box culvert project for the Town of McMillan was excluded from this resolution because it received 90% state funding through the Local Small Structure Improvement Program (LSSIP) for 6-to-20-foot structures, though crews still plan to complete it in October. The committee voted unanimously to forward Resolution 5726 to the county board; the motion was made by Griner and seconded by [inaudible, referred to as 'AC'].

Announcements - North Central Region Fall Legislative Meeting

Kevin announced that the North Central Region fall legislative meeting is scheduled for October 23rd in Stevens Point, featuring a breakfast at 8:30 a.m. with presentations from commissioners and transportation officials, and with all legislators invited. He noted the registration deadline is October 2nd and asked interested committee members to contact him.
