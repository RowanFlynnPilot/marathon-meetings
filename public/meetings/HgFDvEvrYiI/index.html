Minutes from September 8th, 2026

A board member moved to approve the minutes and another seconded. The motion passed unanimously on a voice vote with no discussion.

RFP for Intersection Signal Design Engineering Services

Staff reported that four proposals were received for engineering design services covering signal design at three Bridge Street intersections: from Tadi, Ayres and Associates, Kapoor, and Mead and Hunt. No action was taken; staff indicated the proposals would be reviewed by the engineering department and brought back at a future meeting.

PFAS Treatment — Allas Stone Construction, Change Order No. 8

Staff presented Change Order No. 8 totaling $109,500, described as covering a change in flooring for the PFAS upgrade. Staff noted the change order was reviewed and recommended by consultant Donahue and the water treatment plant superintendent. The presiding officer made the recommendation to approve, a member seconded, and it passed unanimously on a voice vote.

PFAS Treatment — Allas Stone Construction, Pay Estimates No. 18 and No. 19

Staff moved to approve Pay Estimate No. 18 for $148,394.11 and Pay Estimate No. 19 for $377,272.28 together, both reviewed and recommended by consultant Donahue and the water treatment plant superintendent. A member seconded and both pay estimates were approved unanimously on a voice vote.

2026 Asphalt Patching Project — Zilch Asphalt LLC, Pay Estimate No. 5

Staff presented Pay Estimate No. 5 for $1,192.21 covering work completed through September 4th, reviewed and recommended by project inspector Matt Guptail. A motion was made and seconded — with a brief mix-up in order of motion and second — and the pay estimate was approved unanimously on a voice vote.
