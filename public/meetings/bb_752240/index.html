Discussion

WASB Report

Grabko reported on the July legal and legislative update, the WASB summer symposium in Stevens Point which included a legislative recap, school safety discussions, and a presentation by Jesse Crow from the Wisconsin Department of Justice on emerging online safety trends for kids. She also highlighted a new WASB social media campaign called 'Setting the Record Straight' aimed at addressing misconceptions about public education, with its first focus on per-pupil spending.

Superintendent Report

Dr. Knight acknowledged Ellen Suko's name appearing in the employment report, calling her an important district team member without providing further detail. He provided updates on certified staff compensation planning tied to staff survey and town hall feedback, noting that potential insurance increases could limit flexibility; staff will be invited to optional August sessions for feedback with a fall board update planned. He also reported on a facilities study with community engagement meetings beginning with the Village of Weston the following week, and summer projects including ongoing Riverside construction with a 22-week delay on an air handler replacement, new playground equipment at Hatley and Ratchild, and the GTCC cooling and rink project now hosting the World Horseshoe Pitching Championship.

School Handbook Changes

Dr. Knight noted no significant substantive changes to the handbooks, with updates limited to calendars, meal prices, names, and policy updates. Board member Katie Felch raised the idea of implementing an AI chatbot to help families navigate the 37-page handbook more easily, referencing examples from LNS and UW-Stevens Point. Dr. Knight responded positively and suggested connecting with NTC, calling the handbook the most-referenced single resource for families.

First Reading of Policies (Policy 7544 - Social Media)

Larry Schaefer raised a question about Policy 7544 on social media use, specifically whether employees were forbidden from communicating privately one-to-one with students. Felch clarified that the policy prohibits staff from using social media platforms like Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, or X to privately message students, not from using district-approved communication channels like email. Dr. Knight added that the district is being firmer about prohibiting private messaging through social media platforms even when not used inappropriately, and that platforms like Band are the district-approved alternative for group communications.

Set Date for Annual Meeting Budget Hearing

Dr. Strike explained that final budget numbers are available October 15th and the district needs time to adjust before adoption by end of October. The board approved October 28th, 2026 as the date for the annual meeting budget hearing without discussion.

Senior High Trip to Japan

Dr. Knight explained the trip is designed for students engaged in the district's Chinese language program as an opportunity to experience a different part of the world, noting it is not offered every year. The board approved the senior high trip to Japan scheduled for March 2028.
