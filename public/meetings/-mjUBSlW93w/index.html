The Wausau Public Health & Safety Committee held a brief meeting on September 28th, approving minutes, a batch of license applications, and two 90-day liquor license extensions for businesses under construction. Two discussion items — an update on the Marathon County/City of Wausau homelessness response framework and the August tavern activities report — were tabled to the October 19th meeting due to the absence of scheduled presenters.

Discussion

Minutes of preceding meetings

The chair asked if members had reviewed the regular and special Public Health & Safety Committee minutes. No revisions or questions were raised. Alder Slonsky moved to approve and Alder Watson seconded; the motion passed unanimously.

3A: Various license applications

The chair noted no applications were being denied and asked if members were in agreement to approve the batch. No discussion occurred. Alder Watson moved to approve and Alder True Blood seconded; the motion passed unanimously.

3B: 90-day extension for 2510 Delhi and Bakery (2510 Stewart Avenue)

A staff member provided context that the bakery had experienced construction delays but expected to open within the 90-day extension period. No objections were raised. Alder Slonsky moved to approve and Alder Watson seconded; the motion passed unanimously.

3C: 90-day extension for True North #855 (4611 Stewart Avenue)

A staff member noted receiving an email that morning indicating True North expected to be done by November 1st, within the 90-day window. Alder Slonsky moved to approve and Alder True Blood seconded; the motion passed unanimously.

4A: Update on Marathon County and City of Wausau homelessness response framework

The scheduled presenter was absent. Committee members questioned whether the item was related to the homelessness task force and whether the presenter would be Tracy or Megan Bernett, described as the new health coordinator. The chair indicated no motion was needed and the item would be carried over; a committee member suggested asking Alder Larson to place it on the October agenda.

4B: Tavern activities report from August 2026

No presenter was available to report. A committee member noted there were items of potential public interest, including 'pretty intense demerit points' addressed during the month, and suggested circling back next month when staff could be present to answer questions. The item was deferred to the October 19th meeting.
