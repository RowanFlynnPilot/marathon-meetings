Discussion

Text Amendment of Chapter 508 - Water and Sewers

The Plan Commission was scheduled to hold a public hearing on proposed amendments to Chapter 508 subsection 508-37 regarding septic tanks. The amendments were set to establish criteria allowing septic tanks as exceptions in the village's sewer system area, where they are currently prohibited.

The Commission was scheduled to discuss ongoing zoning code review matters as part of old business. Details regarding specific aspects of the code under review were not provided in the agenda.

The Plan Commission was set to take action on septic tank ordinance matters, which relates to the text amendment being considered and was expected to be addressed as new business.

Green Tree Trails Subdivision
