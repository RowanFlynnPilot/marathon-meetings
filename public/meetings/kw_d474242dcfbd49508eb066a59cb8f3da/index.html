The Kronenwetter Village Plan Commission held its November 16, 2026 meeting, approving recommendations on a septic tank ordinance amendment, the Green Tree Trails subdivision final plat, and a stormwater maintenance agreement for the County Road XX distribution center. The commission also received updates on the Pleasant Haven subdivision proposal for the former FAA site, discussed upcoming zoning code review priorities, and flagged traffic safety concerns on Pleasant Road near growing residential development.

Discussion

Motion to Reorder Agenda

The chair moved to shift Item 8G (zoning code review) to after new business items to accommodate the energy levels and public present for those discussions. The motion was seconded and passed 7-0 with no discussion.

Public Hearing: Chapter 508 Septic Tank Ordinance Amendment

No members of the public appeared to speak at the public hearing. The chair announced no sign-ups and closed the hearing without testimony.

Approval of Minutes - July 20 and July 27, 2026

Bruce moved and Dick seconded approval of both sets of meeting minutes. One commissioner noted the minutes lacked a 'prepared by' attribution, which staff acknowledged as an oversight related to catching up after a clerk departure. Minutes were approved 7-0.

Community Development Director Report

Community Development Director Nathan reported on several developments: the DNR notified the village of a Rothschild dam failure analysis requiring updated floodplain maps within roughly a year; the Pleasant Haven subdivision application has been submitted for the former FAA site, requiring both a rezoning and a public hearing likely at the December 21st meeting; and he flagged two zoning code gaps — the lack of a use variance provision and the potential need for zoning changes to enable residential Tax Increment Districts, a new statewide tool. He urged commissioners to confirm December 21st availability soon so a public hearing notice can be published by December 1st.

Chapter 508-37 Septic Tank Ordinance Amendment

Rick Grundman recused himself. The ordinance amendment would allow restricted use of septic tanks within the village sewer service area under specific criteria including a 400-foot distance threshold, minimum 5-acre parcel, and no more than seven months of use per year. A commissioner asked about the basis for those numbers; a commissioner who sits on the utility committee explained they came from DPW Director Greg Almond and the utility foreman, informed by a specific case involving the school's concessions operation. Bruce moved and Dick seconded approval; the motion carried 6-0 (with Grundman recused) and will go to the village board.

Green Tree Trails Subdivision Final Plat and Draft Development Agreement

Nathan reported the final plat conforms with the previously approved preliminary plat from September, with one addition of access detail for the stormwater pond. Developer representative Bill Schofield (Pilgrims Landing, Menomonee Falls) addressed the commission, disclosing several key items: an emergency gated access to Nick Avenue is under contract with PGA Construction, funded by developer J. Shutey; the development is phased over potentially five to seven years with Phase 1 (lots on Pleasant/Pine), Phase 2 (interior lots and road), and Phase 3 (northern section); and Lot 54 is proposed to be sold ahead of its phase due to its proximity to a neighbor's patio, as a goodwill resolution. Commissioners asked about emergency access adequacy; one commissioner with nearly 20 years of emergency services experience said dead-end subdivisions are routinely handled and was not concerned. A commissioner raised traffic safety on Pleasant Road, noting a rear-end accident that evening. Jessica moved and Rick seconded to recommend board approval of the final plat; the motion passed 7-0 on a roll call vote.

Stormwater Maintenance Agreement - 2027 WASA LLC Distribution Center, 1619 County Road XX

Nathan explained the stormwater maintenance agreement fulfills a condition of the site plan approval granted last spring for the large distribution facility on County Road XX. A 170-page stormwater management report was received in September. The agreement ensures ongoing maintenance of the stormwater infiltration system by any future property owner. Bruce moved and Rick seconded to recommend board authorization; the motion passed 7-0.

Zoning Code Review (Item 8G)

Commissioners discussed priorities for zoning code updates. Nathan flagged the lack of a use variance provision (illustrated by a Wilson High School parcel situation involving TID restrictions), driveway clarity, and the need to enable residential TID districts which may require smaller lot size allowances. Commissioners also discussed the potential for mixed-use zoning provisions, noting the existing mixed-use TID district has developed mostly residential. Nathan agreed to compile his running list of code issues for the next meeting agenda. The discussion was informational with no action taken.

Future Agenda Items

A commissioner raised traffic and pedestrian safety concerns on Pleasant Road/Camp Phillips Road, citing a rear-end accident that evening and the lack of a pedestrian pathway, noting that Green Tree Trails and Pleasant Haven will add significant new residents to the area. The group suggested raising this at the board level, researching any existing county traffic studies, and engaging county board members. The appeals process for conditional use permits was also mentioned as a code review item. No formal action was taken.
