Approval of September 9th meeting minutes

Supervisor N[inaudible] moved approval of the minutes; Chair Gibbs seconded. No discussion occurred. The motion carried on a voice vote with no opposition noted.

2027 Annual Budget presentation

County Administrator Leonard presented a 2027 budget he described as 'status quo' in programs and services, with the tax rate dropping 1 cent to $3.48 per $1,000 of equalized value while the average county property tax bill rises approximately $52.58 to $936.92 due to equalized value growth of 6.25%. He highlighted continued funding for homelessness emergency shelter ($150,000), childcare workforce expansion ($50,000 reduced level), a minimum revenue guarantee for Central Wisconsin Airport ($150,000 tied to a $950,000 federal air service development grant requiring local match), and $100,000 for PC replacements. He emphasized the cornerstone of the budget is a funding plan for a new highway shop using $30 million in designated highway reserves and $3.5 million in North Central Healthcare revenues routed through the capital plan, avoiding new borrowing. He also noted one-step wage advancement and a 1% scale shift for employees, budgeted 8% health insurance increase against a 6.5% renewal rate, and no new levy-funded positions. Areas of risk flagged included health insurance costs, high-cost social services placements (one Level 5 treatment foster care placement alone can exceed $595,000/year), and competitive labor market pressures.

2027 Capital Improvement Project funding plan presentation

Deputy Administrator Holman walked through the CIP funding plan, noting total traditional CIP projects of approximately $7.175 million plus $3.5 million for the highway facility and $7 million in bituminous resurfacing. Highlights included increased sheriff's office fleet funding of $595,424 to catch up on 2026 and implement 2027 replacements, new election tabulator equipment for the county clerk, library elevator modernization, courthouse annex roof replacement, LVP (Lake View Professional Plaza) underground piping and interior updates, air handler replacement design near the jail, and an Adele Olson-area parking lot paving project timed to coincide with nearby highway work. He noted approximately $1 million in tax levy and nearly $900,000 in sales tax fund the capital plan, with six TID closures generating roughly $700,000 in additional operating levy capacity redirected to capital.

Department-by-department budget summary

Deputy Administrator Sam [last name inaudible] reviewed all county departments, noting notable changes including: the District Attorney's office restructured staffing to save roughly $20,000 while absorbing loss of discovery fee revenue from a state rate change; the Medical Examiner abolished the deputy chief medical examiner position; Social Services restructured rent calculations and indirect cost allocations; the Sheriff's office unfunded several positions and utilized jail assessment funds; North Central Healthcare submitted a flat levy request; and a new 'state special charges' budget line of just under $6,000 was established for civil mental health commitment proceedings in other counties. ADRC, CCITC, and Birth to Three were the only support agencies receiving funding increases.

Motion to accept the 2027 administrator's proposed budget

Chair Robinson outlined the remaining budget calendar and invited questions. Vice Chair P[inaudible] moved to accept the budget; Chair Gibbs seconded. No substantive discussion was recorded before the vote. The motion carried unanimously on a voice vote. Chair Robinson asked supervisors to direct questions in advance of the October 7 committee meeting and noted outstanding items including the Rib Mountain deputy contract still being finalized.
