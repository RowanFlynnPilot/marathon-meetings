Discussion

September 29th, 2026 Regular Board of Public Works Minutes

A motion was made and seconded to approve the minutes. The board approved unanimously with no discussion.

Wastewater Treatment Facility Screening Improvements – JF Ahern Change Order No. 3

A board member requested a brief description of what the change order covered before voting. Staff explained it included a deduct of approximately $9,900 for removal of an ADA ramp at Structure 100, plus additions for influent screen disconnects and water hammer arresters, resulting in a net increase of $7,354. The board approved the change order unanimously.

2026 Asphalt Patching Project – Zilla Pay Estimate No. 6

Staff noted the pay estimate had been reviewed by Matt Guptail and recommended approval. The amount of $5,062.82 [as stated in transcript] was moved, seconded, and approved unanimously with no significant discussion.

Washington Street Retaining Wall – Yonke Contract General Contractors Pay Estimate No. 4

Staff noted the project was reviewed and overseen by TJ. The pay estimate of $22,710.35 was approved, with a $5,000 retainage being held. Approved unanimously.

2025 Water Treatment Plant Demo – MRD Group Pay Estimate No. 8 and Final Payment

Two separate items were addressed for MRD Group: Pay Estimate No. 8 in the amount of $91,200 and a final payment of $54,441.72. Staff confirmed MRD Group had completed their work and signed the lien waiver. Both items were approved unanimously with minimal discussion.

2025 Street Construction Project A – Ha Suns Inc. Revised Pay Estimate No. 11

This revised pay estimate reflected approved quantities minus $14,000 in liquidated damages for the contractor not meeting completion dates. A board member initially called it a final payment but corrected to 'revised pay estimate.' Staff confirmed the deduction had been negotiated and previously approved by the Board of Public Works. Retainage is still being held. Approved unanimously.

2026 City Hall and Fire Department Concrete Project – Concrete Creations Pay Estimate No. 1

Staff noted the majority of the work is complete and quantities were approved by inspector Brian Per. The pay estimate of $141,922.11 was moved, seconded, and approved unanimously with no significant debate.

2026 Downtown Utility Project – Ha Suns Inc. Change Order No. 2 (Unmarked Post Office Power Line)

Staff explained that during excavation on Second Street near the post office, the contractor struck an unmarked private power line running into the post office. Diggers Hotline had been called but the private line was not marked. Because it was on private property where the city had permission to dig, engineering recommended the city pay the contractor, who had to hire Vanard Electric to repair the post office's internal service. A board member who seconded the motion raised the question of whether the city had a cause of action against the U.S. Postal Service for failing to disclose the line's location, but acknowledged that private lines are not subject to Diggers Hotline requirements. The board approved the $6,246.85 change order unanimously.

2026 Downtown Utility Project – Ha Suns Inc. Pay Estimate No. 4

Staff noted the project is complete and this pay estimate represents final quantities minus retainage, reviewed and approved by Kurt Dbor. The amount of $135,259.52 was approved unanimously.

Closed Session – 2026 Sewer Rehabilitation Project Pre-Qualification Statements

A motion was made to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin Statute 19.85(1)(e) to consider and deliberate on pre-qualification statements for the 2026 sewer rehabilitation project. The motion was seconded and approved unanimously.
