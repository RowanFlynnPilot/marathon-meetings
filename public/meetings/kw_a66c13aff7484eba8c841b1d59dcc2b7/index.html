Police and Fire Commission Meeting

July 27, 2026

The Kronenwetter Police and Fire Commission voted 4-0 to continue investigating a complaint dated June 30, 2026, following a closed session that lasted nearly two hours. The commission also approved changes to the Citizen Charge Form and Citizen Charge Procedures, pending Village Attorney review, in a 4-0 vote. Commissioner Chet Rucinski was absent from the July 27 meeting.

Discussion

Closed Session

Commissioner Rule moved, seconded by Commissioner Udulutch, to convene into closed session under Wis. Stat. § 19.85(1)(f) to consider personnel or disciplinary matters whose public discussion could adversely affect the reputation of the persons involved. The motion carried 3-0 by roll call vote, with the session beginning at 5:03 p.m. Vice President Rick Smith arrived during the closed session at 5:06 p.m. The commission reconvened into open session at 6:46 p.m. on a 4-0 roll call vote, moved by Commissioner Udulutch and seconded by Commissioner Rule.

Action After Closed Session - Complaint dated 06/30/2026

Following the closed session, Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Udulutch, to continue investigating the complaint dated June 30, 2026. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote. No further details about the nature of the complaint were disclosed in open session.

Approval of July 13, 2026 Meeting Minutes

Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Rule, to approve the July 13, 2026 meeting minutes as written. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.

Citizen Charge Form and Procedures

Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Rule, to approve changes to the Citizen Charge Form as submitted, subject to review and approval by the Village Attorney of both the Citizen Charge Form and Citizen Charge Procedures. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.

Action items

  • Commission directed that the investigation of the complaint dated June 30, 2026 continue.
  • Revised Citizen Charge Form and Citizen Charge Procedures approved, subject to Village Attorney review and approval before taking effect.
  • July 13, 2026 meeting minutes approved as written.
