Discussion

Public Comment – Flock License Plate Reader System

Three residents spoke during public comment, all raising concerns about the Flock automated license plate reader (ALPR) system. Felix Gallow of 1206 Highland Park Boulevard presented data alleging widespread surveillance abuse and called on the commission to close data sharing with outside agencies, limit search access to supervisors, require case IDs for every search, and overhaul the audit process. Courtney Olsen, also of 1206 Highland Park Boulevard, identified herself as a domestic violence advocate and police trainer, asking the commission to use its oversight role to request a report on arrests and convictions tied to Flock data, an audit of outside-agency searches, and the department's own camera location list before the city council votes on the $50,000 budget item. Mary CL of 127 Kent Street spoke as a concerned neighbor, citing personal experience with a stalker and expressing discomfort with private companies collecting movement data on residents without clear data security guarantees. The chair acknowledged the comments and noted the topic would be discussed during adjournment.

Consideration of Minutes from Preceding Meeting

A commissioner requested a clarification on page five of the minutes regarding a fire/EMS audit reference to two agencies listed as 'USDA' and 'DEA,' asking which agency conducted the audit and that acronyms be spelled out. The chair confirmed the audit was conducted by the U.S. Drug Enforcement Agency (DEA) and directed that correction be made. The minutes were then approved with that correction on a voice vote.

Motion to Enter Closed Session

The commission voted to enter closed session under Wisconsin State Statute 19.85(1)(c) to interview two candidates for firefighter/paramedic with the Wausau Fire Department and one candidate for promotion to detective with the Wausau Police Department, and under 19.85(1)(f) to consider a reconsideration request regarding the dismissal of a citizen complaint identified as JV. The motion passed on a voice vote.
