Discussion

August 25th Meeting Minutes

A board member moved to approve the minutes and another seconded. The motion passed on a voice vote with all members voting aye. No discussion was recorded.

Pleasant View Park Ash Timber Harvest — Open Bid

One bid was received from Schreiber Logging. The board noted the bid would be reviewed by staff members identified as John and Jamie. No vote on award was recorded in this session.

2026 Sidewalk Repair Project A — Pembrooke Companies Pay Estimate #2

A board member noted the work has been completed and moved to approve the payment of $102,282.35. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously on a voice vote.

2026 Asphalt Paving Project A — RC Pavers Change Order #2

A board member, identified as John, explained that a crosswalk at Packer Drive had not been included in the original plan and a contractor was brought in to repaint it, resulting in a change order of $354.38. The motion to approve was made, seconded, and passed unanimously.

2026 Asphalt Paving Project A — RC Pavers Pay Estimate #4

A board member moved to approve the pay estimate of $103,122.41. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously on a voice vote with no notable discussion.

2025 Water Treatment Plant Demo — MRD Group Pay Estimates #6 and #7

The board approved two pay estimates totaling $399,755.25. A board member remarked that the site looks 'really different down there,' suggesting visible demolition progress. The motion passed unanimously.

2025 Downtown Mall Redevelopment Phase 2 — Pembrooke Companies Pay Estimate #9

A board member explained this represents near-final quantities, with $10,000 in retainage held pending concrete sealing work expected to be completed before fall. Once sealing is done, the $10,000 will be released. The payment of $233,403.56 was approved unanimously.

Portland Cement Concrete Licenses — ABG Masonry and A1 Western Masonry

A board member, identified as John, stated he reviewed both license applications and bonds and moved to approve both. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously.

Closed Session — Deliberating on Claims

A board member moved to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin State Statute 19.85(1)(g) to deliberate on claims. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously on a voice vote.
