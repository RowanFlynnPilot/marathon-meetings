The Village of Weston Plan Commission held its September 14, 2026 meeting, addressing three conditional use permit compliance cases involving USM Storage (3107 Concord Ave), Bug Properties (6406 Schofield Ave), and Tommy's Car Wash (1611 Schofield Ave), as well as approving two subdivision/plat items. Public Comment - Items Not on Agenda

Jim Pinsol (address not stated) submitted a written public comment read into the record criticizing inconsistencies in how staff handled the three CUP violation cases, alleging bias, lack of accountability, missing timelines, and gross negligence regarding Tommy's Car Wash being in a wellhead protection area for five years without required documentation. He also called on any commissioner with financial ties to affected properties to recuse themselves. Online commenter Jake (last name not stated), owner of townhomes at 314/318 Ross Avenue, read a letter detailing his concerns about USM Storage expanding gravel and RV parking into green space beyond permit boundaries, water runoff erosion on his property, and the precedent set by owner Mr. Minell repeatedly making unpermitted improvements.

Camp Phillips Road Condominium Plat (Project 20260315)

Staff presented a preliminary/final condominium plat for 4603-4613 Camp Phillips Road, where Lockree Development is purchasing the Vendor family property to establish a four-unit condominium plat. A representative from Lockree Development explained the road and stormwater pond would be common elements and the arrangement would function similarly to the existing rental use. Staff reported no revisions needed and recommended approval subject to eight conditions. The commission approved the plat unanimously.

Amended Final Plat - Bold Junior View Subdivision (Project 20250424)

Staff explained the original final plat approved in July 2026 was never recorded, and the Wisconsin DNR subsequently required a 60-foot public access easement to Bull Junior Creek affecting lots 12 and 13, making them slightly smaller. Since the plat was unrecorded, the developer amended it to incorporate the easement and returned for reapproval. One commissioner asked whether all six conditions in the packet should be included; staff clarified they would all appear on the village board resolution, with condition four (public access easement) being the only new addition. The commission approved the amended plat unanimously.

CUP Compliance - USM LLC, 3107 Concord Avenue

Staff reported USM Storage was parking RVs and trailers in a designated green space and had added gravel, expanding beyond what the approved site plan showed. Property owner Steve Minell argued the site was in compliance with the April 2024 CUP approval, stating vehicles in the green space had been moved by August 19-20 and that gravel maintenance was not new expansion. Online neighbor Jake disputed this, stating new gravel extended roughly 12 feet into the green space. Commission members debated at length that the approved site plan lacked dimensional specifications for the green space buffer, making enforcement subjective. Ultimately the commission agreed a modification was needed to establish a clear numerical setback, and voted to direct staff to commence modification proceedings and schedule a public hearing rather than finding the owner definitively non-compliant.

CUP Compliance - Bug Properties, 6406 Schofield Avenue

Staff presented detailed findings showing the property at 6406 Schofield Avenue had not been completed per the approved site plan, with a CUP completion deadline of October 9, 2025 having passed nearly a year prior. Outstanding items included concrete work, fencing, gates, landscaping, and curbing; the building permit had also expired. Staff reported owner Bruce Gus had not responded to emails or phone calls, with his voicemail box full. Commissioners expressed frustration at the lack of response and agreed the September 23, 2026 compliance deadline in the motion was generous. The commission voted to find the property non-compliant and set a September 23 deadline, directing staff to schedule a public hearing at the October 12 plan commission meeting if not resolved.

CUP Compliance - Tommy's Car Wash, 1611 Schofield Avenue

Staff reported that Tommy's Car Wash had failed to provide required annual underground sediment tank inspection reports and a certificate of environmental insurance for multiple consecutive years, with only a 2021 report on file. Staff noted the facility is located in a wellhead protection area and that outreach since January 2026 had yielded no documentation and no response. Commissioners expressed strong concern about the wellhead protection implications, with one member arguing this case warranted more urgency than a green space dispute. The commission voted to find the CUP out of compliance and directed staff to send written notice with a September 23, 2026 deadline, with the matter returning to the commission for further action if not resolved.
