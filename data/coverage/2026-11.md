## How to read this

- **No regular schedule on file** for City of Wausau, Village of Kronenwetter. Every meeting the board posted is counted, but the tracker has no calendar to check them against, so a meeting it never saw would not show up here.
- **No agenda source** for Marathon County: the tracker has no way to read this board's agendas, so nothing is shown before its meetings beyond the date and time.
- The record of agendas read before meetings starts on November 23, 2026. For a meeting held before that, "agenda before" is known only where the meeting's card was made from its agenda.
- **A regular meeting with no card** may have been cancelled or moved, or its card may be worded differently from the schedule. The tracker does not guess; check the board's own calendar.
- **Closed sessions** are never recorded and are not counted as waiting or overdue.
- **Tracked but not yet published:** Mosinee School Board, City of Mosinee, Village of Rothschild, Village of Maine. Left out; run with --include-hidden to check them.
