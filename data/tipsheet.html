Reporter tip sheet: week of Tuesday, Nov 24

Central Wisconsin Meeting Tracker. Sorted from the tracker's summaries and agenda previews; software wrote those, so confirm with the record before reporting.

Decisions coming up

  • Tuesday, Nov 24, 12:00 PM: City of Wausau, Community Development Authority Board Meeting (residents can speak; closed session)
    • VOTE: Seven 2027 budgets for housing programs and facilities. The board is asked to vote on separate budgets for the Housing Choice Voucher Program, Public Housing Program, Riverview Terrace, Riverview Towers LLC, Fulton Street, and 8th Avenue.
    • VOTE: Repositioning public housing scattered sites to voucher program. The board is asked to vote on Resolution #26-012 to convert scattered public housing sites to project-based vouchers after a consultant presents repositioning options.
    • VOTE: Riverview Towers façade repair bids. The board is asked to discuss and vote on bids received for masonry and concrete façade repairs at Riverview Towers LLC.
    • VOTE: Sub-recipient agreement for 405 S 8th Avenue property. The board is asked to discuss and vote on a rental property agreement between the city and the authority for 405 S 8th Avenue.
    • VOTE: Capital needs assessment for scattered sites properties. The board is asked to discuss and vote on a request for proposals for a capital needs assessment of scattered public housing sites.
  • Tuesday, Nov 24, 5:15 PM: City of Wausau, Finance Committee Meeting (residents can speak)
    • VOTE: Animal impoundment services agreement with Humane Society. The committee will vote to approve a contract with the Humane Society of Marathon County for the city to use their animal impoundment services.
    • VOTE: Five other service and equipment purchase agreements. The committee will vote to approve agreements for mapping software (Esri), county production services, cemetery operations, and fire department cardiac monitors and ventilators.
    • VOTE: Budget change and lift purchase for Athletic Park. The committee will vote to approve a budget modification and sole source request to buy an outdoor vertical platform lift for Athletic Park.
  • Tuesday, Nov 24, 6:30 PM: City of Wausau, Common Council Meeting (residents can speak; closed session)
    • VOTE: 2026 budget modification for year-end projections. The Finance Committee is asking the council to approve various budget adjustments based on how the year ended.
    • VOTE: Salary structure revision for general city employees. The Finance Committee and Human Resources Committee are asking the council to revise how general employees' salaries are structured.
    • VOTE: Special assessments for 2027 street construction projects. The Infrastructure & Facilities Committee is asking the council to levy special assessments (charges to property owners) to pay for street work in 2027.
    • VOTE: Deed restrictions and non-conforming uses at 1300 Cleveland Avenue. The Economic Development Committee is asking the council to approve deed restrictions and clarify existing non-conforming uses on city-owned property.
    • VOTE: Driveway and concrete approach exceptions for Kolbe & Kolbe Millwork. The Infrastructure & Facilities Committee is asking the council to approve oversized driveways and waive a concrete approach requirement at three nearby addresses on South 11th Avenue.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 10:30 AM: City of Wausau, Board of Public Works Meeting
    • VOTE: Recommendation for 2026 demolition of 207 North 1st Avenue. The board will decide whether to recommend demolition of the building at 207 North 1st Avenue (MBX South). Bids were opened on November 17.
    • VOTE: Pay estimates for lift station and street projects. The board will approve four contractor payments: a second payment for Crocker Street Lift Station work, final payment for Cherry Street Lift Station, first payment for downtown sidewalk improvements, and final payment for asphalt paving project.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 11:30 AM: City of Wausau, Water Works Commission Meeting
    • VOTE: PFAS settlement funds for capital projects. The commission is scheduled to vote on approving how to use settlement money for water system improvements.
    • VOTE: Lead service line replacement fund borrowing. The commission is scheduled to vote on borrowing money from the regular drinking water funding program to replace lead service lines on the public side of connections.
    • VOTE: Carbon regeneration of water treatment vessels. The commission is scheduled to vote on approving Carbon Activated Corporation to regenerate six more activated carbon vessels used to treat drinking water.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 5:30 PM: Mosinee School Board, Student Expulsion Hearing (closed session)
    • HEARING: Student expulsion hearing. The board will hold a closed hearing to consider expulsion of a student under Wisconsin law.
