Decisions coming up

  • Monday, Nov 30, 5:30 PM: Village of Maine, Board of Trustees Meeting (residents can speak; closed session)
  • Monday, Nov 30, 6:00 PM: Village of Maine, Plan Commission (residents can speak)
    • HEARING: Amendment to Ordinance §17.08-5: Accessory Uses and Structures. The commission will hear public comments on proposed changes to rules governing accessory structures, then vote on whether to approve the amendment.
    • HEARING: Amendment to Ordinance §17.13: Principal Building Lot Coverage. The commission will hear public comments on proposed changes to rules about how much of a lot a main building can occupy, then vote on whether to approve the amendment.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 10:30 AM: City of Wausau, Board of Public Works Meeting
    • VOTE: Recommendation for 2026 demolition of 207 North 1st Avenue. The board will decide whether to recommend demolition of the building at 207 North 1st Avenue (MBX South). Bids were opened on November 17.
    • VOTE: Pay estimates for lift station and street projects. The board will approve four contractor payments: a second payment for Crocker Street Lift Station work, final payment for Cherry Street Lift Station, first payment for downtown sidewalk improvements, and final payment for asphalt paving project.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 11:30 AM: City of Wausau, Water Works Commission Meeting
    • VOTE: PFAS settlement funds for capital projects. The commission is scheduled to vote on approving how to use settlement money for water system improvements.
    • VOTE: Lead service line replacement fund borrowing. The commission is scheduled to vote on borrowing money from the regular drinking water funding program to replace lead service lines on the public side of connections.
    • VOTE: Carbon regeneration of water treatment vessels. The commission is scheduled to vote on approving Carbon Activated Corporation to regenerate six more activated carbon vessels used to treat drinking water.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 5:15 PM: City of Wausau, Economic Development Committee Meeting (residents can speak)
    • VOTE: EPA technical assistance for 1300 Cleveland Avenue redevelopment. The committee is asked to approve a scope of work for EPA OBLR (Brownfields) Land Revitalization Technical Assistance providing site visioning and financing support for 1300 Cleveland Avenue.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 5:30 PM: Mosinee School Board, Student Expulsion Hearing (closed session)
    • HEARING: Student expulsion hearing. The board will hold a closed hearing to consider expulsion of a student under Wisconsin law.
  • Tuesday, Dec 1, 7:00 PM: Mosinee School Board, Special School Board Meeting (residents can speak; closed session)
  • Wednesday, Dec 2, 4:30 PM: City of Wausau, Historic Preservation Commission Meeting
    • VOTE: Historic Preservation Commission webpage. The commission will discuss and vote on creating a webpage to inform residents and historic property owners about preservation rules and information.
  • Wednesday, Dec 2, 7:30 AM: City of Wausau, Police & Fire Commission Special Meeting (closed session)
  • Thursday, Dec 3, 5:00 PM: City of Wausau, Sustainability, Energy & Environment Committee Meeting (residents can speak)
    • VOTE: Approving title for Five-Year Sustainability Plan. The committee will vote on a name for the city's comprehensive five-year sustainability plan.
