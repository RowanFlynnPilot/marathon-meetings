Discussion

Excellence in Action: Riverview Elementary School

The board was scheduled to receive a presentation from Riverview Elementary School highlighting school leadership team initiatives and Habit 3 announcements. The presentation materials were included in the agenda packet.

4K Program Agreement

Kara Rakowski, 4K Principal and Early Childhood Coordinator, and Julie Schell, Director of Elementary Education, were scheduled to present and request board approval for 2027-2028 Program Agreements with community partners. The presentation was expected to cover partnerships with current sites including Wausau Child Care, Mountain View Montessori, and Newman Catholic Schools - St. Michael, as well as re-engagement options with Woodson YMCA - Wausau Branch and Marathon County Child Development Agency Head Start Program - Barrington Center.

Staff Turnover Report

Tabatha Gundrum was scheduled to present a turnover and retention report for the district's employee groups, with an estimated presentation time of 10 minutes. The presentation was expected to discuss areas the district will address in the coming year based on specific information from the report.

Neola Update

The board was expected to receive a comprehensive policy update from Neola Vol. 35 No. 2, which included general updates to 24 policies covering curriculum, student activities, assessment, professional growth, technology, health services, food services, and transportation, along with technical corrections and district-initiated updates to additional policies.

2026-2027 Student Demographic Report

Ralph Williams, Director of Technology, was scheduled to present the Demographics Report for the 2026-2027 school year, with an estimated presentation time of 10 minutes.

Referendum Budget Summary Updates

Dr. Elizabeth Channel, Assistant Superintendent of Operations, was scheduled to provide an update on the Referendum Budget with an estimated presentation time of 10 minutes.

Secondary Social Studies Update

The board was expected to receive a presentation on an adjusted 6-12th grade scope and sequence for secondary social studies. The presentation was scheduled to include an overview of the process, adjustments made, and timeline, with an estimated presentation time of 10 minutes.

Action items
