The Marathon County Board of Supervisors held an educational meeting on October 15, 2026, primarily focused on a detailed presentation of Phase 4 construction plans for the proposed county highway facility, including a guaranteed maximum price of approximately $54.98 million. The board also previewed upcoming voting items for the next meeting, including multiple town rezoning ordinances, ARPA fund reallocation, and a land purchase near Cherokee Park. No formal votes were taken at this educational meeting, as action items are deferred to the October 20 HR Finance and Property Committee meeting and the subsequent full County Board meeting.

Discussion

Standing Committee Announcements

Supervisor Robinson announced the HR Finance and Property Committee would meet the following Tuesday before the full County Board meeting, with the agenda including funding for the highway facility resolution, a resolution declaring November as Project Greenlight Month for veterans, and a resolution expressing concerns about proposed U.S. Census rule changes that could reduce Wisconsin's congressional representation by counting winter residents in southern states.

Town Wind, Solar, and Data Center Ordinances (9B2A–9B2C)

Supervisor Lemmer raised concerns about the county's shift to a purely compliance-based review of town ordinances rather than substantive legal review, questioning whether this created liability risk. Corporation counsel explained the shift was intended to limit the county's liability since towns that have opted out of county zoning must enforce their own ordinances; the county's role under state statute is only to verify procedural compliance. Supervisor Sandowski asked whether the Town of Wausau data center ordinance addressed hyperscale facilities, and was reminded that the town's ordinance applies only within its own boundaries.

Cherokee Park Land Purchase (Resolution 66-26)

Supervisor Jensen asked about the status of Knowles-Nelson Stewardship Fund funding referenced in the resolution; Administrator Landon and Supervisor Robinson explained there is currently no funding in the program but the reference was included so the county could pursue it if reauthorized. Supervisors Hagen and Jensen asked about the need for the land, its tax impact, and ongoing maintenance costs; Parks Director Jamie Paully explained the parcel is currently forested, generates minimal tax revenue, and would complete the park boundary along the river with minimal added maintenance unless future trail development is pursued.

Phase 4 Highway Facility Construction Presentation

Deputy Administrator and Facilities Director Chris Holman led an extensive presentation alongside project architect Ned Dirksen, Miron project executive Eric Brandt, and Excel Engineering's Corey Powers on geothermal HVAC, air quality filtration, value engineering savings, site and floor plans, and the guaranteed maximum price of $53.3 million in direct construction costs, with a total project figure of $54,980,537 including contingencies, design fees, and owner FF&E. Supervisors asked detailed questions about geothermal maintenance costs, MERV filter expenses, coal dust impacts on workers and equipment, backup heating systems, truck traffic flow, expansion capacity, LEED-equivalent design standards, and the competitive bid process; Holman and the project team answered that the bore field cost $1.9 million with a $760,000 IRA tax credit anticipated, that backup gas boilers are included for temperatures below zero, and that the guaranteed maximum price represents a ceiling with potential to come down. Supervisor Griner asked what percentage of the building would be immediately utilized, and Commissioner [inaudible] confirmed that all existing equipment including 82 large pieces, 24 plow trucks, and over 100 small tools would fill the main building, with current equipment routinely stored outside and degrading. The board was informed the resolution authorizing Phase 4 will be considered at the HR Finance and Property Committee on October 20 and then the full County Board that same evening.
